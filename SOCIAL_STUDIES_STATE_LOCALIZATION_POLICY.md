# Khaemenes Academy Social Studies · State Localization Policy

**Policy:** Every Khaemenes Academy Social Studies grade is state-localizable.

The curriculum uses a **national-core / state-local-overlay** model:

1. The national disciplinary core remains canonical.
2. State/local examples, sources, maps, biographies, institutions, Indigenous histories, economic cases, civic structures, and public issues are integrated wherever developmentally appropriate.
3. Localized material must be evidence-based and current where current facts matter.
4. Localization may add or substitute examples/case studies, but may not delete required disciplinary competencies or weaken assessment rigor.
5. Formal progression remains governed by the Academy's 80% mastery policy.
6. Local civic/election work remains nonpartisan.
7. Learners are never required to disclose sensitive personal or family information to make a lesson local.
8. Indigenous localization should name specific nation(s) where possible, use present-day continuity, and prefer nation/tribal sources when available.
9. A state/local overlay should be documented in the grade's `STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`.
10. When a state's Grade-level sequence differs sharply from the Khaemenes national spine, the overlay should satisfy the state requirement **without narrowing the broader Academy course**.

This policy applies to current grades and all future Social Studies development.
