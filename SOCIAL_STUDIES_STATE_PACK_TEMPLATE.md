# Khaemenes Academy Social Studies · Universal State Pack Template

All K–12 Social Studies state packs use the same fields: geography; specific Indigenous nations/present-day communities; local/state chronology; government/constitution; civic institutions; African American and other local contributors; economy; archives/museums; student-facing source sets; and checkpoint substitutions.

A state pack adds jurisdiction-specific evidence to the canonical national course. It may not lower the 80% mastery threshold or remove national disciplinary competencies.
