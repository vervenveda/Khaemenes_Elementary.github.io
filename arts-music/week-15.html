Weather and Seasonal Patterns

weather symbols and soundscape

Monday: Visual Art: weather symbols and soundscape

Objective: Students build visual art skill through weather symbols and soundscape within the weekly theme Weather and Seasonal Patterns.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for weather symbols and soundscape.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing visual art progress.
