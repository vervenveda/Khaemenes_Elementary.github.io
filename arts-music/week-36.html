Arts & Music · Week 36

Final Review and Celebration

final arts portfolio

Monday: Visual Art: final arts portfolio

Objective: Students build visual art skill through final arts portfolio within the weekly theme Final Review and Celebration.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for final arts portfolio.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing visual art progress.
