Simple Color Theory

Primary Colors

Red, yellow, and blue are traditional primary colors in elementary art instruction.

Warm & Cool

Warm colors often include reds, oranges, and yellows. Cool colors often include blues, greens, and violets.

Contrast

Light/dark and complementary-like color differences can make parts of an artwork stand out.

Pattern Samples

A pattern repeats a visual rule. Try line, shape, color, spacing, or alternating forms.
