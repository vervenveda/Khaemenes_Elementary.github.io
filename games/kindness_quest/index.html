🧭 Think Like a Kind Leader

Before acting, consider these questions:

  • Is this safe and welcome?
  • Did I ask before helping or touching belongings?
  • Does this solve a real need?
  • Can the other person say no?
  • Am I helping without taking over?

🌎 Mini Service Project Planner

For Grades 2–5, families, or classrooms. Plan a small project with adult guidance.

Kindness and service safety: Students should ask an adult before helping strangers, sharing food, handling animals, collecting donations, cleaning litter, going outdoors, carrying heavy objects, or contacting organizations. Kindness never requires unwanted touch, secrecy, unsafe work, personal information, or giving away belongings without permission.
