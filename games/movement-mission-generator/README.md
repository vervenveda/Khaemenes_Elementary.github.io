# Movement Mission Generator 🏃

**Khaemenes Elementary · Grades 1–5 · 36-Week PE**

Movement Mission Generator is a sovereign, local-first elementary physical-education companion that generates short **3–7 minute movement missions**.

It focuses on:

- balance
- pathways
- rhythm
- coordination
- counting
- spatial awareness
- safe movement choices
- reflection
- teamwork / communication
- whole-child wellness

It intentionally does **not** score children by body size, weight, speed, endurance, strength, appearance, or comparative fitness.

## Safety and inclusion rules

The app repeatedly reinforces:

- move only in clear space
- choose a comfortable range of motion
- slow down whenever needed
- substitute a movement anytime
- avoid body-comparison scoring
- avoid ranking children by speed, strength, or endurance
- treat time as a session timer, not a fitness score
- focus on participation, attention, control, and reflection
