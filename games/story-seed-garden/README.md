# Story Seed Garden 🌱📖 — K–5

A standalone interdisciplinary Khaemenes app combining plant-life science, sequencing, observation, writing, handwriting, data, and storybook creation.

## Core learning cycle
**Plant → Care → Observe → Measure → Sequence → Explain → Write → Retell**

## Grade progression

### Kindergarten
- seed, root, stem, leaf
- basic plant needs
- picture sequencing
- first / next / last
- oral retelling
- drawing and large-line writing

### Grade 1
- plant part functions
- water and light
- beginning / middle / end
- simple sentence retelling
- before / after language

### Grade 2
- life cycles
- conditions for growth
- cause and effect
- prediction vs. observation
- evidence sentences

### Grade 3
- environmental effects
- chronological paragraphs
- measurement
- observation reports
- simple pattern analysis

### Grade 4
- structures and resource needs
- variables
- repeated observations
- experiment comparisons
- explanatory writing
- evidence-based conclusions

### Grade 5
- plant-growth models
- controlled investigation thinking
- data interpretation
- claims and evidence
- competing explanations
- limitations and experimental improvement

## Major modes
- Plant Lab
- Story Sequencing Studio
- Observe & Explain
- Write & Draw
- Growth Data
- My Plant Storybook
- Portfolio

## Plant model
The simulation changes visible root, stem, leaf, flower, height, and leaf-count outcomes according to:
- day
- water
- light

It explicitly identifies itself as a simplified learning model rather than a universal biological prediction.

## Handwriting integration
Younger grades receive large guideline rows and sentence starters.
Older grades transition toward independent science writing.
The drawing/writing canvas uses the same local touch/mouse/stylus logic developed for Letter & Handwriting Garden.

## Data
Each advanced day can save:
- day
- water
- light
- modeled height
- modeled leaf count

A simple growth chart is generated from recorded observations.

## Storybook
The app automatically assembles saved observations and simulation data into a printable Plant Storybook.
