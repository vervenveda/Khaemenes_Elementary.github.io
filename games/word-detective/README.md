# Word Detective 🔎

**Khaemenes Elementary · Grades 1–5 · Language Arts**

Word Detective is a sovereign, local-first word-analysis game in which learners earn **clues rather than points**.

The app focuses on:

- prefixes
- suffixes
- vowel teams
- syllables
- compound words
- r-controlled vowels
- nouns
- verbs
- adjectives

It is especially appropriate for second-grade Weeks 2, 5, 14, and 27–28, while scaling across Grades 1–5.

## AuroraCore inspiration

The Aurora Language Refinement Core describes a modular language pipeline with grammar correction, thesaurus replacement, syllable-aware rhythm, mood/cohesion, case preservation, and deterministic processing.

For Word Detective, the most useful ideas are not automatic rewriting. They are the **language-analysis concepts** behind the engine:

- modular word/language processing
- syllable awareness
- grammar awareness
- bounded transformations
- deterministic behavior for repeatable activities
- preservation of the learner's original wording

Word Detective therefore uses AuroraCore as inspiration for analysis, not for silently rewriting a child's work.

## Core gameplay

### Case File
Each case presents a word and asks the learner to identify a useful clue.

### Highlighting
The learner selects meaningful chunks of the word.

### Clues, not points
Correct reasoning earns a clue. The game avoids leaderboard-style scoring.

### Detective Notes
Learners record what they noticed.

### Explain the Clue
Learners explain how the word part, sound pattern, or grammatical role helped them.

### Sort Evidence
Learners classify words as nouns, verbs, or adjectives.

### Story Clue
Short sentences and story snippets provide context-based language investigation.

### Case Portfolio
Saved cases preserve word evidence, learner notes, and explanations.

### Grade 3
- multisyllabic decoding
- morphology
- affixes
- context clues
- increasingly complex grammar

### Grade 4
- derivational morphology
- more advanced affix/root awareness
- multisyllabic analysis
- grammar choices affecting meaning

### Grade 5
- advanced morphology
- root/affix relationships
- complex syllable structures
- nuanced word class
- vocabulary relationships
- context-driven analysis
