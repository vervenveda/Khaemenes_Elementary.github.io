# Grade 01 Curriculum Map

## Week 01: Welcome to First Grade Scholars
- Essential question: How do first graders learn with courage, kindness, and careful work?
- Literacy: names, classroom print, listening, speaking, sentence frames
- Math: count, compare, sort, and explain strategies
- Inquiry: classroom community, routines, maps, and responsibilities
- SEL: self-awareness, courage, and asking for help
- Project: My First Grade Scholar Shield

## Week 02: Letters, Sounds, and Counting to 20
- Essential question: How do letters, sounds, and numbers help us explain what we notice?
- Literacy: letter names, consonant sounds, short vowel listening, sight words
- Math: count to 20, one more, one less, number order
- Inquiry: noticing patterns in classroom tools and books
- SEL: focus, turn-taking, and careful listening
- Project: Alphabet and Number Pattern Book

## Week 03: Short Vowels and Addition Stories
- Essential question: How can sounds and number stories help us read and solve?
- Literacy: short a and e words, blending, beginning/middle/end
- Math: addition within 10 using objects and drawings
- Inquiry: family stories and classroom examples
- SEL: patience when a task is difficult
- Project: Short Vowel Story Mat

## Week 04: More Short Vowels and Subtraction Stories
- Essential question: How do readers and mathematicians notice what changes?
- Literacy: short i, o, and u words, CVC decoding, rereading
- Math: subtraction within 10, take apart, compare
- Inquiry: observing changes in simple situations
- SEL: trying again after mistakes
- Project: Change Story Flipbook

## Week 05: Families, Communities, and Teen Numbers
- Essential question: How do people and numbers work together in groups?
- Literacy: characters, setting, retell, questions about text
- Math: teen numbers as ten and some more
- Inquiry: families, helpers, rules, and community roles
- SEL: respect and responsibility
- Project: Community Helper Number Mural

## Week 06: Weather, Seasons, and Data
- Essential question: How can we use words, numbers, and pictures to describe weather?
- Literacy: informational text features, weather words, oral report
- Math: collect data, picture graphs, compare more/less
- Inquiry: weather observations and seasonal patterns
- SEL: safe choices and flexible thinking
- Project: Class Weather Graph

## Week 07: Plants, Living Things, and Measurement
- Essential question: How do living things grow and how can we measure change?
- Literacy: main idea, details, labels, science vocabulary
- Math: measure length with nonstandard units
- Inquiry: plant parts, needs, growth, observation
- SEL: care, patience, and stewardship
- Project: Seed Observation Journal

## Week 08: Animals, Habitats, and Classification
- Essential question: How do animals use body parts and habitats to survive?
- Literacy: compare texts, animal facts, category words
- Math: sort and classify by attributes
- Inquiry: habitats, animal needs, life cycles
- SEL: curiosity and careful questions
- Project: Animal Habitat Diorama

## Week 09: Authors, Illustrators, and Addition Strategies
- Essential question: How do authors and mathematicians choose good strategies?
- Literacy: author/illustrator purpose, story details, fluency
- Math: addition strategies: count on, doubles, make ten
- Inquiry: creative choices in books and art
- SEL: confidence and sharing work
- Project: Strategy Story Poster

## Week 10: Folktales, Lessons, and Subtraction Strategies
- Essential question: What lessons can stories and number problems teach?
- Literacy: folktales, moral/lesson, sequence, expression
- Math: subtraction strategies: count back, take away, compare
- Inquiry: traditions and storytelling
- SEL: honesty and reflection
- Project: Folktale Lesson Puppet Show

## Week 11: Maps, Shapes, and Our School World
- Essential question: How do maps and shapes help us describe where things are?
- Literacy: direction words, captions, map vocabulary
- Math: 2D and 3D shapes, attributes, compose shapes
- Inquiry: maps, symbols, school/community locations
- SEL: belonging and cooperation
- Project: Classroom Map and Shape City

## Week 12: Needs, Wants, Money, and Choices
- Essential question: How do people make careful choices with resources?
- Literacy: opinion sentence, reason, economics vocabulary
- Math: coins, values, compare amounts, solve simple money stories
- Inquiry: needs/wants, goods/services, saving/sharing
- SEL: gratitude and responsible choices
- Project: Needs and Wants Market

## Week 13: Matter, Properties, and Careful Observation
- Essential question: How can we describe objects like scientists?
- Literacy: adjectives, labels, precise describing words
- Math: measure and compare length, weight, capacity informally
- Inquiry: properties of matter: color, texture, shape, use
- SEL: careful observation and safety
- Project: Properties Investigation Board

## Week 14: Light, Sound, and the Senses
- Essential question: How do our senses help us learn about the world?
- Literacy: sensory words, informational details, asking questions
- Math: patterns, data, compare observations
- Inquiry: light, sound, senses, cause/effect
- SEL: mindful attention and safety
- Project: Sound and Light Lab

## Week 15: Time, Calendars, and Sequence
- Essential question: How do we organize events in time?
- Literacy: transition words, beginning/middle/end, journal writing
- Math: time to hour/half hour, calendar patterns
- Inquiry: daily schedules, past/present/future
- SEL: planning and independence
- Project: My Day Timeline

## Week 16: Review, Revision, and Portfolio Growth
- Essential question: How does revision help our learning grow?
- Literacy: rereading, revising sentences, reading fluency review
- Math: mixed addition/subtraction review within 20
- Inquiry: review science/social studies evidence
- SEL: growth mindset and pride
- Project: Midyear Portfolio Preparation

## Week 17: Reading for Information and Two-Digit Numbers
- Essential question: How do facts and numbers help us explain clearly?
- Literacy: nonfiction features, facts/opinions, note-taking
- Math: two-digit numbers, tens and ones, compare numbers
- Inquiry: research a classroom question
- SEL: focus and scholarly habits
- Project: Fact Card Museum

## Week 18: Midyear Mastery Review
- Essential question: What have we learned, and how can we show it with evidence?
- Literacy: reading comprehension, phonics review, oral reading
- Math: number sense, addition/subtraction, shapes, data
- Inquiry: integrated review across communities, plants, weather, matter
- SEL: celebration, reflection, and next steps
- Project: Midyear Learning Gallery

## Week 19: Long Vowels and Silent E
- Essential question: How can one letter change a word?
- Literacy: silent e, long a/e/i/o/u patterns, word families
- Math: add/subtract within 20 with unknowns
- Inquiry: word patterns in poems and songs
- SEL: noticing details
- Project: Silent E Word Garden

## Week 20: Blends, Digraphs, and Adding Within 100
- Essential question: How do small parts combine to make bigger ideas?
- Literacy: blends and digraphs, chunking, fluency
- Math: add two-digit and one-digit numbers with models
- Inquiry: building with parts and wholes
- SEL: persistence and strategy choice
- Project: Blend Builder and Number Bridge

## Week 21: Opinion Writing and Clear Reasons
- Essential question: How do we share an opinion respectfully?
- Literacy: opinion, reason, closing sentence, peer listening
- Math: compare numbers and solve opinion-survey data
- Inquiry: class preferences and civic voice
- SEL: respectful disagreement
- Project: Opinion Mini-Book

## Week 22: How-To Writing, Measurement, and Directions
- Essential question: How do steps help others learn something new?
- Literacy: how-to writing, sequence words, clear directions
- Math: measure, compare, order lengths
- Inquiry: tools, directions, safe procedures
- SEL: helpfulness and clarity
- Project: How-To Demonstration Card

## Week 23: Earth, Sky, Sun, Moon, and Stars
- Essential question: What patterns do we observe in the sky?
- Literacy: question words, science reading, diagram labels
- Math: calendar patterns, data, time of day
- Inquiry: sun/moon/stars, day/night, shadows
- SEL: wonder and careful recording
- Project: Sky Pattern Journal

## Week 24: Forces, Motion, and Problem Solving
- Essential question: How do pushes and pulls change motion?
- Literacy: cause/effect, action verbs, explanation sentences
- Math: measurement data, compare distances, solve problems
- Inquiry: push, pull, speed, direction
- SEL: safe exploration
- Project: Ramp and Motion Investigation

## Week 25: Citizenship, Rules, and Responsibility
- Essential question: How do responsible choices help a community?
- Literacy: realistic fiction, character choices, evidence
- Math: multi-step story problems within 20
- Inquiry: rules, rights, responsibilities, fairness
- SEL: integrity and service
- Project: Class Constitution Poster

## Week 26: Cultures, Traditions, and Family Stories
- Essential question: How do stories help us understand people?
- Literacy: personal narrative, culture words, listening to stories
- Math: patterns and comparison using real-life data
- Inquiry: traditions, holidays, foods, music, family history
- SEL: appreciation and kindness
- Project: Family Story Quilt

## Week 27: Poetry, Music, and Patterns
- Essential question: How do rhythm and patterns help us remember?
- Literacy: rhyme, rhythm, poetry, expressive reading
- Math: number patterns, skip counting, repeated addition pictures
- Inquiry: music, movement, and memory
- SEL: creative courage
- Project: Poetry and Pattern Performance

## Week 28: Fractions, Shapes, Halves, and Fourths
- Essential question: How can we divide shapes and objects fairly?
- Literacy: describing parts, precise math language, sentence frames
- Math: halves/fourths, equal shares, partition shapes
- Inquiry: fair sharing and design
- SEL: fairness and patience
- Project: Equal Share Art

## Week 29: Research Writing and Expert Topics
- Essential question: How do we become experts and teach others?
- Literacy: research question, facts, headings, drawing/labeling
- Math: organize data and explain findings
- Inquiry: choose an animal/place/tool topic
- SEL: focus and responsibility
- Project: First Grade Expert Page

## Week 30: Problem-Solving Strategies and Mathematical Reasoning
- Essential question: How do we choose a strategy and explain why it works?
- Literacy: explain thinking in words, listen and respond
- Math: mixed story problems, drawings, equations, unknowns
- Inquiry: real classroom problem solving
- SEL: courage to revise
- Project: Strategy Choice Board

## Week 31: Ecosystems, Stewardship, and Care
- Essential question: How can we care for living things and places?
- Literacy: main idea/details, stewardship vocabulary
- Math: data about living things, compare and graph
- Inquiry: ecosystems, needs, care, human choices
- SEL: kindness toward living things
- Project: Care for a Place Plan

## Week 32: Inventors, Tools, and Design Thinking
- Essential question: How do people design tools to solve problems?
- Literacy: biography basics, problem/solution, design vocabulary
- Math: measure, compare, and improve designs
- Inquiry: inventors, tools, simple machines, prototypes
- SEL: creativity and persistence
- Project: Helpful Tool Prototype

## Week 33: Stories Around the World
- Essential question: What can stories from many places teach us?
- Literacy: compare stories, setting, characters, lesson
- Math: map story places, compare distances informally
- Inquiry: world cultures, maps, respect
- SEL: open-mindedness
- Project: World Story Passport

## Week 34: Leadership, Service, and Community Action
- Essential question: How can first graders help their community?
- Literacy: persuasive sentence, service vocabulary, speech practice
- Math: plan with numbers, count supplies, compare totals
- Inquiry: community service and leadership
- SEL: service and gratitude
- Project: Class Service Project

## Week 35: Portfolio Polish and Student Conferences
- Essential question: How can we show our best work and explain our growth?
- Literacy: reading/writing portfolio review, conference speaking
- Math: mixed math review with explanation
- Inquiry: select evidence from science/social studies
- SEL: pride and honest reflection
- Project: Student-Led Conference Folder

## Week 36: Final Review and Grade Two Readiness
- Essential question: How are we ready for the next step?
- Literacy: full literacy review, independent reading response
- Math: full math review, reasoning and accuracy
- Inquiry: integrated science/social studies review
- SEL: celebration and readiness
- Project: Grade Two Readiness Gallery
