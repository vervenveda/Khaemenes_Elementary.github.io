# Upload Notes

1. Download and unzip this package.
2. In GitHub, open `vervenveda/Khaemenes_Elementary.github.io`.
3. Back up the current `grades/grade-01/` folder or keep a copy locally.
4. Upload/merge the included `grades/grade-01/` folder.
5. Test:
   - `grades/grade-01/index.html`
   - `grades/grade-01/lessons/unit-01/index.html`
   - `grades/grade-01/assessments/unit-01-assessment.html`
   - `grades/grade-01/assessments/midterm.html`
   - `grades/grade-01/assessments/final-exam.html`
   - `grades/grade-01/records/certificate.html`

Recommended first test:
- Take Week 01 quiz.
- Score below 80: Week 02 remains locked.
- Score 80 or higher: Week 02 opens.
- Continue to verify localStorage works after refresh.
