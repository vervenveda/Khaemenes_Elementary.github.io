Discovery to Independence

Five gentle Grade 1 lessons building on preschool play. All activities are instructional practice, not graded tests. Students may respond by speaking, pointing, writing or drawing. Existing weekly mastery exams remain separate.

Monday · Read, Retell, Wonder

Read a short familiar story with an adult. Identify the beginning, middle and end. Ask what the character learned.

Show your thinking: Draw three events in order. Retell them in your own words.

Enter Week 01

Tuesday · Count, Explain, Check

Collect ten safe counters. Make two groups, count each group, combine them and verify by counting a second time.

Show your thinking: Show 4 + 3 = 7 with objects, a picture and one sentence.

Practice in Week 01

Wednesday · Observe Like a Scientist

Choose an ordinary object such as a leaf or stone. Look closely, describe texture and shape, and ask one testable question.

Show your thinking: Create a labeled observational drawing; distinguish what you saw from what you guessed.

Visit Science

Thursday · Think About Our Community

Describe ways people share a classroom or public space. Discuss fairness with a fictional story, not personal disclosures.

Show your thinking: Draw a classroom community rule and explain who it helps.

Visit Social Studies

Friday · Make and Share

Combine reading, numbers, observation and kindness in a small original poster. Discuss how your work changed during revision.

Show your thinking: Present, point to or write about one example of learning and one goal for next time.

Continue the Learning Path

Mentor guide · Check understanding without a test

Before each activity, model one example aloud. Invite the student to try independently, then ask: “What did you notice? How could you check?” Accept speaking, drawing, pointing, or writing.

Support: Use two or three objects or picture cards, repeat the directions and offer sentence starters such as “I noticed…” and “I think… because…”.

Extension: Ask the student to create a new example, explain two approaches, or identify what additional evidence would help.

Family observation: Note one strength, one question and one next-step activity on paper or in the existing weekly note. This companion makes no changes to assessment grades or stored mastery.
