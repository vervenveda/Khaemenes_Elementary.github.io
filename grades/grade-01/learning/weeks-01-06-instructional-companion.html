Weeks 01–06 · Explore and Explain

Optional offline-friendly instructional extensions. Use the existing lesson pages for saved work, weekly quizzes and certificates; this guide does not store grades or alter mastery.

Week 01 · Belonging and observation

Read and discuss: Read a short classroom story; name two details and explain how a character feels.

Mathematical reasoning: Sort six objects by a shared attribute, then regroup them using a different rule.

Inquiry: Draw something noticed in the classroom; label what was observed versus guessed.

Accessible demonstration: The learner may speak, point, draw or write. Ask for an explanation rather than speed; provide a model, then fade support.

Return to Week 01 lesson

Week 02 · Patterns and prediction

Read and discuss: Read a repeating-pattern poem; identify repeated words and predict the next line.

Mathematical reasoning: Build two color or shape patterns; explain the repeating unit and find an error.

Inquiry: Observe a daily routine and predict what comes next, then check the prediction.

Accessible demonstration: The learner may speak, point, draw or write. Ask for an explanation rather than speed; provide a model, then fade support.

Return to Week 02 lesson

Week 03 · Counting and evidence

Read and discuss: Retell a story in three ordered events; explain which illustration supports each event.

Mathematical reasoning: Represent numbers to 20 with objects, drawings and a number line; compare two strategies.

Inquiry: Count objects outdoors or in a picture and record the method used.

Accessible demonstration: The learner may speak, point, draw or write. Ask for an explanation rather than speed; provide a model, then fade support.

Return to Week 03 lesson

Week 04 · Questions and investigation

Read and discuss: Read a short information passage and write or dictate one question not answered by it.

Mathematical reasoning: Solve a word problem using counters, a picture and a number sentence.

Inquiry: Ask a testable question about a safe object, observe and record one result.

Accessible demonstration: The learner may speak, point, draw or write. Ask for an explanation rather than speed; provide a model, then fade support.

Return to Week 04 lesson

Week 05 · Community and fairness

Read and discuss: Compare two characters' choices and describe a respectful solution to a disagreement.

Mathematical reasoning: Use addition or subtraction to fairly share classroom materials; explain the reasoning.

Inquiry: Create a community-help poster with a clear claim and supporting detail.

Accessible demonstration: The learner may speak, point, draw or write. Ask for an explanation rather than speed; provide a model, then fade support.

Return to Week 05 lesson

Week 06 · Synthesis and revision

Read and discuss: Revise a three-sentence story for clearer sequence and descriptive language.

Mathematical reasoning: Invent and explain a two-step number story; check the solution another way.

Inquiry: Present one small investigation with a question, observation and conclusion.

Accessible demonstration: The learner may speak, point, draw or write. Ask for an explanation rather than speed; provide a model, then fade support.

Return to Week 06 lesson
