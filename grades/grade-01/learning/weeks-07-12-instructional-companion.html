Weeks 07–12 · Practice, Reason, Explain

Supplementary offline-friendly instruction only. Use the established lesson pages for saved work, grades, assessments and certificates. This page stores no learner information.

Week 07 · Reading for meaning

Read and express: Read an illustrated passage and distinguish a stated fact from a reasonable inference.

Math and reasoning: Compare numbers within 20 with a drawing and an explanation.

Investigate: Observe one plant or object over two days and distinguish change from uncertainty.

Support: Model once, provide picture cards or counters, then invite independent oral, drawn or written explanations.

Stretch: Ask the learner to offer a second strategy and say what new evidence could change an answer.

Return to Week 07

Week 08 · Patterns in words and numbers

Read and express: Identify a sound or word pattern and explain how it helps reading.

Math and reasoning: Build and extend repeating and growing patterns, then find a deliberate mistake.

Investigate: Track daily weather with simple symbols and explain one pattern.

Support: Model once, provide picture cards or counters, then invite independent oral, drawn or written explanations.

Stretch: Ask the learner to offer a second strategy and say what new evidence could change an answer.

Return to Week 08

Week 09 · Measurement and description

Read and express: Choose precise descriptive words for a familiar object; revise one sentence.

Math and reasoning: Measure two objects using identical nonstandard units; explain why equal units matter.

Investigate: Compare observations and discuss fairness in a measurement method.

Support: Model once, provide picture cards or counters, then invite independent oral, drawn or written explanations.

Stretch: Ask the learner to offer a second strategy and say what new evidence could change an answer.

Return to Week 09

Week 10 · Story problems and solutions

Read and express: Retell a character's challenge, evidence and solution in order.

Math and reasoning: Model addition and subtraction word problems using objects and number sentences.

Investigate: Test two possible solutions to a classroom design challenge.

Support: Model once, provide picture cards or counters, then invite independent oral, drawn or written explanations.

Stretch: Ask the learner to offer a second strategy and say what new evidence could change an answer.

Return to Week 10

Week 11 · Communities and maps

Read and express: Read a simple map key and explain a safe route between landmarks.

Math and reasoning: Use position words and draw a grid with clear labels.

Investigate: Compare the needs of two fictional community members respectfully.

Support: Model once, provide picture cards or counters, then invite independent oral, drawn or written explanations.

Stretch: Ask the learner to offer a second strategy and say what new evidence could change an answer.

Return to Week 11

Week 12 · Explain, check, revise

Read and express: Read a short informational text, name its main idea, and cite two details.

Math and reasoning: Solve a two-step age-appropriate number story and independently verify.

Investigate: Present a small evidence portfolio with a claim, observation and question.

Support: Model once, provide picture cards or counters, then invite independent oral, drawn or written explanations.

Stretch: Ask the learner to offer a second strategy and say what new evidence could change an answer.

Return to Week 12
