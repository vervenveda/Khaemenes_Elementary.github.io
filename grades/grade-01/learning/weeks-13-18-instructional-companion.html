Weeks 13–18 · Build, Explain, Reflect

Optional offline instructional support. Weekly quizzes, mastery thresholds, student records, and certificates remain in the established Academy lessons.

Week 13 · Place value and number stories

Language arts: Read a short account and identify the sequence and main idea.

Mathematics: Represent tens and ones with bundles, pictures and numbers; explain regrouping with objects.

Inquiry: Record how many objects are in two groups and explain the counting method.

Support: Model with objects or pictures, then ask for an independent explanation orally, in writing, or through drawing.

Extension: Invite a second solution or a test of the learner’s assumption.

Return to Week 13

Week 14 · Describing change

Language arts: Compare a character or setting before and after an event, citing evidence.

Mathematics: Solve a change problem with subtraction and verify using addition.

Inquiry: Observe how light or temperature changes a safe everyday setting.

Support: Model with objects or pictures, then ask for an independent explanation orally, in writing, or through drawing.

Extension: Invite a second solution or a test of the learner’s assumption.

Return to Week 14

Week 15 · Shapes, space and precision

Language arts: Describe a familiar object using two precise attributes.

Mathematics: Compose and decompose simple two-dimensional shapes; justify the composition.

Inquiry: Sketch a simple map and explain positions with directional vocabulary.

Support: Model with objects or pictures, then ask for an independent explanation orally, in writing, or through drawing.

Extension: Invite a second solution or a test of the learner’s assumption.

Return to Week 15

Week 16 · Informational reading and questions

Language arts: Identify a nonfiction text's topic, two details and a question it leaves open.

Mathematics: Compare lengths using identical units and explain why the unit must be consistent.

Inquiry: Test a safe question using repeated observations and distinguish facts from guesses.

Support: Model with objects or pictures, then ask for an independent explanation orally, in writing, or through drawing.

Extension: Invite a second solution or a test of the learner’s assumption.

Return to Week 16

Week 17 · Time and daily routines

Language arts: Read a daily schedule, retell the order and identify transition words.

Mathematics: Sequence and compare durations with clocks or daily event cards.

Inquiry: Create and revise a responsible classroom routine with others' needs in mind.

Support: Model with objects or pictures, then ask for an independent explanation orally, in writing, or through drawing.

Extension: Invite a second solution or a test of the learner’s assumption.

Return to Week 17

Week 18 · Midyear synthesis and reflection

Language arts: Draft and revise a short explanatory paragraph with two supporting details.

Mathematics: Solve a real-world number challenge using two representations and independently check.

Inquiry: Share an inquiry claim, evidence and an open question; reflect on progress.

Support: Model with objects or pictures, then ask for an independent explanation orally, in writing, or through drawing.

Extension: Invite a second solution or a test of the learner’s assumption.

Return to Week 18
