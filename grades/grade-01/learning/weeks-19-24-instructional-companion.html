Weeks 19–24 · Observe, Solve, Explain

Optional instructional material for students, teachers and families. Existing weekly quizzes, saved progress, mastery gates and certificates remain independent of this page.

Week 19 · Comparing stories and explanations

Language arts: Read two short accounts of the same event and compare what each narrator notices.

Mathematics: Represent two-digit numbers with tens and ones; compare their values and explain why.

Inquiry: Observe the same safe object from two viewpoints and record what changes.

Support: Demonstrate with pictures or objects, allow spoken or drawn answers, and offer a sentence starter.

Extension: Ask the learner to defend a different method, identify an uncertainty, or design a new example.

Return to Week 19

Week 20 · Addition strategies and evidence

Language arts: Identify the main idea and two supporting details in an informational passage.

Mathematics: Solve two-digit addition with drawings, place-value blocks, and a number line; verify another way.

Inquiry: Use a simple tally table to answer a classroom question and note missing information.

Support: Demonstrate with pictures or objects, allow spoken or drawn answers, and offer a sentence starter.

Extension: Ask the learner to defend a different method, identify an uncertainty, or design a new example.

Return to Week 20

Week 21 · Subtraction and problem solving

Language arts: Sequence a character’s problem, decision and consequence; suggest another solution.

Mathematics: Model subtraction with tens and ones, checking results using addition.

Inquiry: Test two solutions to a simple design problem and explain the stronger one.

Support: Demonstrate with pictures or objects, allow spoken or drawn answers, and offer a sentence starter.

Extension: Ask the learner to defend a different method, identify an uncertainty, or design a new example.

Return to Week 21

Week 22 · Living things and habitats

Language arts: Read a short nonfiction text; distinguish a fact from a prediction.

Mathematics: Sort and count observations using a tally chart; compare totals.

Inquiry: Observe a plant or a habitat photograph and explain what living things need, without disturbing wildlife.

Support: Demonstrate with pictures or objects, allow spoken or drawn answers, and offer a sentence starter.

Extension: Ask the learner to defend a different method, identify an uncertainty, or design a new example.

Return to Week 22

Week 23 · Money, choices and fairness

Language arts: Compare two fictional characters' purchasing choices and support an opinion.

Mathematics: Make amounts using common coins or paper models and check totals.

Inquiry: Discuss needs and wants through a respectful fictional shop activity without collecting family financial details.

Support: Demonstrate with pictures or objects, allow spoken or drawn answers, and offer a sentence starter.

Extension: Ask the learner to defend a different method, identify an uncertainty, or design a new example.

Return to Week 23

Week 24 · Explaining what we know

Language arts: Write or dictate a short paragraph with a claim and two supporting details.

Mathematics: Create a word problem with a two-digit number, solve and verify it.

Inquiry: Prepare a small portfolio showing a question, observation, conclusion and a next question.

Support: Demonstrate with pictures or objects, allow spoken or drawn answers, and offer a sentence starter.

Extension: Ask the learner to defend a different method, identify an uncertainty, or design a new example.

Return to Week 24
