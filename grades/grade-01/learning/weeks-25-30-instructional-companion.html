Weeks 25–30 · Investigate and Communicate

Optional offline-friendly practice; existing quizzes, grading and learner records remain the official progress systems.

Week 25 · Evidence and explanations

Reading and writing: Find the main idea and cite two details from a short nonfiction text.

Mathematics: Represent a two-digit number in several ways and explain equivalence.

Inquiry: Observe a pattern in everyday objects and distinguish observation from explanation.

Support: Offer pictures, manipulatives and sentence stems. Invite oral, drawn or written responses.

Challenge: Explain a second method or identify evidence that might change an answer.

Return to Week 25

Week 26 · Graphs and questions

Reading and writing: Read captions and labels, then summarize what a simple chart communicates.

Mathematics: Collect a small, nonpersonal tally data set and create a picture graph.

Inquiry: Ask which question the graph answers and which it cannot answer.

Support: Offer pictures, manipulatives and sentence stems. Invite oral, drawn or written responses.

Challenge: Explain a second method or identify evidence that might change an answer.

Return to Week 26

Week 27 · Geometry around us

Reading and writing: Compare two descriptions and revise for accurate shape vocabulary.

Mathematics: Find, sort, compose and draw two- and three-dimensional shapes.

Inquiry: Explain why objects may have similar shapes but different purposes.

Support: Offer pictures, manipulatives and sentence stems. Invite oral, drawn or written responses.

Challenge: Explain a second method or identify evidence that might change an answer.

Return to Week 27

Week 28 · Time and measurement

Reading and writing: Write steps for a familiar routine in clear chronological order.

Mathematics: Compare times and lengths using consistent units; show the measurement method.

Inquiry: Repeat a safe measurement and explain why results might differ.

Support: Offer pictures, manipulatives and sentence stems. Invite oral, drawn or written responses.

Challenge: Explain a second method or identify evidence that might change an answer.

Return to Week 28

Week 29 · Working together

Reading and writing: Read two viewpoints about a fictional classroom decision and identify shared goals.

Mathematics: Plan a small supply-sharing problem using addition and subtraction.

Inquiry: Discuss a fair, practical solution and explain how people can revise it.

Support: Offer pictures, manipulatives and sentence stems. Invite oral, drawn or written responses.

Challenge: Explain a second method or identify evidence that might change an answer.

Return to Week 29

Week 30 · Independent inquiry

Reading and writing: Write a short explanation with a claim, evidence and a concluding sentence.

Mathematics: Invent a problem involving place value or measurement and verify its solution.

Inquiry: Choose a safe question, record observations and state a limitation.

Support: Offer pictures, manipulatives and sentence stems. Invite oral, drawn or written responses.

Challenge: Explain a second method or identify evidence that might change an answer.

Return to Week 30
