Weeks 31–36 · Apply, Reflect, Advance

Supplementary offline-friendly instruction. Formal grades, progress, weekly quizzes, and certificates remain in the existing mastery system.

Week 31 · Reflecting on change

Reading and writing: Read two short accounts and compare how a situation changed.

Mathematics: Represent a two-digit calculation in objects, drawings and equations.

Inquiry: Explain how observations support a conclusion and identify a remaining question.

Support: Offer manipulatives, illustrated prompts, and spoken or drawn responses before independent writing.

Extension: Invite a second solution, critique or reflective question.

Return to Week 31

Week 32 · Planning a helpful project

Reading and writing: Draft instructions for a small classroom service project with a clear sequence.

Mathematics: Estimate and count supplies; check totals and compare two options.

Inquiry: Design a safe project plan including roles, materials and a backup approach.

Support: Offer manipulatives, illustrated prompts, and spoken or drawn responses before independent writing.

Extension: Invite a second solution, critique or reflective question.

Return to Week 32

Week 33 · Reading and interpreting information

Reading and writing: Read a simple informational passage and identify its main idea and two evidence details.

Mathematics: Interpret a picture graph and identify what the data cannot prove.

Inquiry: Compare two observation records without making assumptions about people.

Support: Offer manipulatives, illustrated prompts, and spoken or drawn responses before independent writing.

Extension: Invite a second solution, critique or reflective question.

Return to Week 33

Week 34 · Problem-solving showcase

Reading and writing: Write a short fictional challenge and revise its ending with a reasoned solution.

Mathematics: Create and solve a two-step number or measurement problem; verify with a second method.

Inquiry: Present a drawing or demonstration explaining a test and its limitations.

Support: Offer manipulatives, illustrated prompts, and spoken or drawn responses before independent writing.

Extension: Invite a second solution, critique or reflective question.

Return to Week 34

Week 35 · Portfolio reflection

Reading and writing: Select two pieces of work and explain how each demonstrates improvement.

Mathematics: Compare early-year and current strategies, noting greater accuracy or clarity.

Inquiry: Describe a question that changed after collecting evidence.

Support: Offer manipulatives, illustrated prompts, and spoken or drawn responses before independent writing.

Extension: Invite a second solution, critique or reflective question.

Return to Week 35

Week 36 · Readiness for Grade 2

Reading and writing: Read a grade-appropriate story and explain cause, effect and character choices.

Mathematics: Use place value, operations, shapes and measurement in a mixed practical task.

Inquiry: Describe one area of confidence and a realistic next step; celebrate curiosity over speed.

Support: Offer manipulatives, illustrated prompts, and spoken or drawn responses before independent writing.

Extension: Invite a second solution, critique or reflective question.

Return to Week 36
