Unit 22

Animals, Parents, and Offspring

Animal structures, survival, parent/offspring traits, observation, and animal reports

Essential Question

How do animals grow, survive, and resemble their parents?

Activity: Introduce the essential question, vocabulary, read-aloud or decodable text, discussion routine, and anchor chart.
