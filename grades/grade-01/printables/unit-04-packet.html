Printable Packet · Week 04

More Short Vowels and Subtraction Stories

Essential Question: How do readers and mathematicians notice what changes?

Weekly Focus

Literacy: short i, o, and u words, CVC decoding, rereading

Math: subtraction within 10, take apart, compare

Inquiry: observing changes in simple situations

Project: Change Story Flipbook

Vocabulary: decode, subtract, change, compare, reread, explain

Five-day evidence prompts

Monday · Launch, Listen, and Read

Objective: I can decode short i, o and u words and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Build sit, hop, sun and cup with movable letters and match them to simple illustrations.

Evidence to keep: CVC sound-change strip. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.

Student work / adult observation: _____________________________________________________

Tuesday · Skill Practice and Number Talk

Objective: I can model subtraction as taking away and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Solve 9−4 and 7−2 with counters and verify each by addition.

Evidence to keep: Original take-away story with a checked equation. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.

Student work / adult observation: _____________________________________________________

Wednesday · Inquiry Workshop

Objective: I can compare before and after observations and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Make a before/after record showing observed counts, a subtraction equation and a cautious explanation.

Evidence to keep: Labeled observation and change diagram. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.

Student work / adult observation: _____________________________________________________

Thursday · Create, Write, and Explain

Objective: I can publish a Change Story Flipbook and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Draw, write or dictate a three-page story with a short-i/o/u word and subtraction within ten.

Evidence to keep: Complete Change Story Flipbook. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.

Student work / adult observation: _____________________________________________________

Friday · Review, Reflect, and Mastery Check

Objective: I can independently verify decoding and subtraction and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Read unseen CVC words, solve a new take-away problem and explain how you verified it.

Evidence to keep: Independent decoding and corrected subtraction work. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.
