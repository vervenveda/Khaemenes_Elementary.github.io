Printable Packet · Week 05

Families, Communities, and Teen Numbers

Essential Question: How do people and numbers work together in groups?

Weekly Focus

Literacy: characters, setting, retell, questions about text

Math: teen numbers as ten and some more

Inquiry: families, helpers, rules, and community roles

Project: Community Helper Number Mural

Vocabulary: community, helper, teen, ten, retell, role

Five-day evidence prompts

Monday · Launch, Listen, and Read

Objective: I can name characters, setting and community helper roles and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Retell two important events in a different community story, citing an actual story detail.

Evidence to keep: Character, setting and helper evidence card. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.

Student work / adult observation: _____________________________________________________

Tuesday · Skill Practice and Number Talk

Objective: I can represent teen numbers as a ten and extra ones and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Model 13, 15 and 18, label each full ten and the extra ones.

Evidence to keep: Accurate teen number drawing and labels. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.

Student work / adult observation: _____________________________________________________

Wednesday · Inquiry Workshop

Objective: I can explain a community responsibility and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Choose a library, park or neighborhood role and explain one realistic helpful action.

Evidence to keep: Respectful role-and-action explanation. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.

Student work / adult observation: _____________________________________________________

Thursday · Create, Write, and Explain

Objective: I can design a Community Helper Number Mural and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Draw another helper and a number from 11–19; show the number as 10 plus extra ones.

Evidence to keep: Community Helper Number Mural with 10+n model. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.

Student work / adult observation: _____________________________________________________

Friday · Review, Reflect, and Mastery Check

Objective: I can retell a story and independently model teen numbers and explain what proves my answer.

Independent task: Retell three ordered events and represent a different teen number with a ten-frame.

Evidence to keep: Portfolio retelling and ten-and-ones demonstration. Ask the student to point to, say or write the evidence for the conclusion.
