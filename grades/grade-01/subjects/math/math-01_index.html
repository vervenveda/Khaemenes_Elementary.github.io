Welcome!

This portal generates a 42-week curriculum (210 weekdays). Each school week contains 5 days of 10 simple problems aimed at 1st grade skills: counting, addition/subtraction within 20, missing addends, simple comparisons, and a few word problems. Weekly quizzes (every 5th day) review the week's skills. Midterm at week 21 and a Final at week 42 are longer (20–30 problems) and include printable answer sheets.

How to use: choose a week and day on the left, click "Start Today's 10 Problems". Use the worksheet button to open a printable PDF-style page for the selected day. When a child completes a day (checks answers), confetti will play.
