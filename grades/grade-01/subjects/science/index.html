36-week learning path

A year of discovering how the world works

This course begins with noticing, asking questions, and describing evidence. Learners investigate living things, materials, motion, sound, light, weather, land, water, and simple design challenges through age-appropriate activities.

Mastery approach: practice, feedback, correction, and another attempt are part of learning. The Academy's 80% mastery target applies to essential checks; a finished activity alone does not certify mastery.

How each week works

1 · Wonder

Notice a phenomenon, ask a focused question, and make a prediction before investigating.

2 · Learn

Build vocabulary and a simple model with teacher or caregiver support.

3 · Investigate

Observe, compare, sort, measure when appropriate, or work with supplied data.

4 · Explain

Use drawings, words, objects, numbers, or an oral explanation to show evidence.

5 · Reflect

Check understanding, revise an idea, and save or print a learning sample.

Safe science

No tasting unknown materials or mixing household products. Use adult help for tools, heat, electricity, and fieldwork.

Weekly learning map

Choose a week to open its lesson plan. Printable and offline-friendly learning are supported; experiments can be replaced with observation or supplied data when needed.

Evidence portfolio

Keep a small science portfolio with a question, prediction, drawing or data, what was observed, and what the evidence suggests. Include what changed in your thinking. A grown-up may scribe the learner's own words.

The weekly pages are instructional plans. This landing page does not claim that a learner has completed or mastered any week.
