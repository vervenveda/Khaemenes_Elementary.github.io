Week 31: Planning a safe investigation

Essential question: What should we plan before we investigate?

Course overview Previous week

Five-day learning plan

Monday · Wonder

Choose a safe question and list materials.

Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and what the evidence suggests.

Tuesday · Learn

List ordered steps, observations, and precautions.

Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and what the evidence suggests.

Wednesday · Investigate

Find missing steps in a sample plan.

Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and what the evidence suggests.

Thursday · Design

Write or draw a step-by-step plan and ask an adult to check it.

Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and what the evidence suggests.

Friday · Explain

A checked plan with question, steps, observation method, and safety note.

Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and what the evidence suggests.

Access and extension

Foundation: use picture choices and adult scribing. Core: complete the evidence record. Extension: compare a second example or propose another test.

Vocabulary

plan · material · step · safe

Evidence notebook

Safety: A trusted adult must approve materials and steps before practical work. Use photographs or supplied data if materials or supervision are unavailable. Never taste unknown materials or mix household products.

Reflection

Explain the question, show the evidence, and describe how the evidence supports your idea. Revise after feedback. The Academy's 80% target applies to reviewed assessments and correction/reassessment, not this reflection.
