Week 32: Weather, Safety, and Community Decisions

Essential question: How can weather information help us make safe choices?

Course overview Previous week Next week

Five-day learning plan

Monday · Wonder

Compare sunny, rainy, windy, and stormy pictures.

Evidence prompt: Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and explain how the evidence supports your idea.

Tuesday · Learn

Match conditions with sensible precautions.

Evidence prompt: Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and explain how the evidence supports your idea.

Wednesday · Investigate

Use a supplied forecast card to discuss a safe clothing or activity choice.

Evidence prompt: Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and explain how the evidence supports your idea.

Thursday · Design

Create a weather-safety card for a classroom or family.

Evidence prompt: Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and explain how the evidence supports your idea.

Friday · Explain

Support one decision with the supplied weather information.

Evidence prompt: Draw, say, write, or show what you noticed and explain how the evidence supports your idea.

Vocabulary: forecast · condition · precaution · evidence

Safety: During severe weather, follow official alerts and trusted adult instructions. Use photographs or supplied data if suitable materials or supervision are unavailable.

Evidence notebook

Foundation: use picture choices and adult scribing. Core: record evidence and explain it. Extension: compare another example or propose another test.
