Week 33: Earth Materials and Changing Surfaces

Essential question: How can water and wind change Earth's surface?

Course overview Previous week Next week

Monday · Wonder

Compare before-and-after photographs of a slope or stream bank. Draw what looks different.

Tuesday · Learn

Model erosion as movement of material by water or wind. Use a drawing to show where material begins and where it moves.

Wednesday · Investigate

Identify where material moved in a teacher-provided image sequence. Mark evidence directly on the image or in a table.

Thursday · Design

Draw a safe model barrier that could slow pretend runoff, then explain what evidence would show whether it helped.

Friday · Explain

Share an annotated model and a sentence that connects the change in the picture to water or wind.

Vocabulary: erosion · surface · soil · move

Safety: Prefer a paper model; an adult supervises any water trays and cleanup. Use photos or supplied data if practical work is unsuitable.

Evidence notebook

Foundation: use picture choices and adult scribing. Core: record evidence and explain it. Extension: compare a second example or propose another test.
