Khaemenes Academy · Grade 1 Science

Week 34: Integrated Science Design Challenge

Essential question: How can science help solve a small problem?

Course overview Previous week Next week

Monday · Wonder

Choose a low-risk problem, such as keeping a paper picture dry or helping a toy reach a marked place.

Tuesday · Learn

Review relevant knowledge about materials, weather, plants, or motion and choose what matters to the problem.

Wednesday · Investigate

Compare possible solutions using a short criteria list. Use safe tests, pictures, or supplied observations.

Thursday · Design

Build or draw a prototype, test it safely, then improve one feature based on what happened.

Friday · Explain

Present a portfolio page containing the question, model, test evidence, revision, and explanation.

Vocabulary: criteria · prototype · test · revise

Safety: Use adult-approved materials; no heat, chemicals, or electricity. Use pictures or supplied data when practical work is unsuitable.

Evidence notebook

Foundation: use picture choices and adult scribing. Core: record test evidence and explain it. Extension: compare another design or propose another test.

The 80% mastery target applies to reviewed course assessments and correction/reassessment. This page does not automatically certify mastery.
