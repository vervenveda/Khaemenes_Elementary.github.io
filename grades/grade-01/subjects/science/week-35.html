Khaemenes Academy · Grade 1 Science

Week 35: Science Showcase

Essential question: How can we explain what we learned?

Course overview Previous week Next week

Monday · Wonder

Choose an earlier investigation, model, or data display that you want to explain.

Tuesday · Learn

Compare your early idea with the explanation supported by your observations or supplied data.

Wednesday · Investigate

Check your portfolio sample for accurate observations, clear labels, and a claim supported by evidence.

Thursday · Design

Prepare an exhibit or oral explanation that includes a drawing, data display, model, or saved notebook.

Friday · Explain

Share one claim, two evidence details, and a reflection on how your thinking changed.

Vocabulary: portfolio · explain · evidence · reflect

Access: Learners may present orally, visually, in writing, or with caregiver transcription.

Evidence notebook

Foundation: use picture choices and adult scribing. Core: identify evidence and explain it. Extension: compare the strength of two evidence sources or propose a next test.

Safety and access: Use only safe, adult-approved exhibit materials. No new practical investigation should begin without appropriate supervision. Learners may present orally, visually, in writing, or with caregiver transcription.
