Week 36: Year-End Reflection and Next Questions

Essential question: What can our evidence tell us, and what should we investigate next?

Course overview Previous week

Monday · Wonder

Revisit one question from earlier in the year and explain why it interested you.

Tuesday · Learn

Name the observations or data that support your current explanation. Mark which parts are direct observations and which parts are your interpretation.

Wednesday · Investigate

Compare two pieces of evidence and identify a remaining question or something that was not measured.

Thursday · Design

Draw or write a proposal for a next-grade investigation. Name what you would observe and what materials or adult support you would need.

Friday · Explain

Reflect on one learning gain and one new testable question. Share the portfolio with a trusted adult or teacher.

Vocabulary: reflect · evidence · question · investigate

Safety: Any new practical investigation needs age-appropriate materials and adult approval.

Evidence notebook

Foundation: share one artifact with adult scribing. Core: connect a claim to evidence. Extension: name a limitation and design a follow-up test.
