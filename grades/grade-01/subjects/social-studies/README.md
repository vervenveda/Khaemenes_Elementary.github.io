# Khaemenes Academy · Grade 1 Social Studies
## Families, Communities & Our World

Canonical route: `grades/grade-01/subjects/social-studies/index.html`

### Course record
- **state-localizable by design and requirement**; see `STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`
- localization may adapt examples and evidence but **never lowers the 80% mastery gate or removes national core competencies**
- 36 discipline-specific weeks
- 180 Monday–Friday lessons
- 9 four-week units
- 80% weekly mastery unlock policy
- open-book weekly mastery checks
- current-week question banks plus spiral-review items
- closed-book, teacher-supervised randomized midterm after Week 18
- closed-book, teacher-supervised randomized cumulative final after Week 36
- correction and fresh-form reassessment below 80%
- portfolio evidence, local progress, JSON export/import, printing
- no external libraries

The existing integrated Grade 1 mastery curriculum remains intact. This course creates the canonical **Social Studies subject identity** and can use integrated Grade 1 work as evidence where it genuinely addresses the Social Studies objective.

Teacher convenience code: `KHAE1SS`. This is not secure authentication.

### Disciplinary progression
Identity/family/community → civics/rules/democracy → geography/place → history/sources/change → economics/choice → culture/global communities → civic memory/government → inquiry/environment/action → research/capstone/Grade 2 bridge.

### Standards basis
Designed from the NCSS C3 Inquiry Arc and broad Grade 1 patterns in New York, California, Florida, and other state frameworks. National portability does not itself confer accreditation or automatic acceptance by every jurisdiction.

## State/local localization

This course follows the Academy-wide **national-core / state-local-overlay** rule. State and local maps, sources, institutions, biographies, Indigenous histories, public services, economic examples, environmental cases, and community evidence should be integrated where appropriate without removing national competencies or changing the 80% mastery standard.

See `STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`.


### Localization source of truth
`STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md` is the one canonical Grade 1 state/local overlay guide. The national core and 80% mastery gates remain invariant.
