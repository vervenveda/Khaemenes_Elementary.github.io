Teacher Tools

First Grade Mentor Toolkit

Everything here is local, printable, and designed for a rigorous, developmentally appropriate A+ first grade program.

01

Pacing Guide

Use one unit per week, five lessons per unit. Preserve time for reading groups, math centers, writing workshop, movement, play, and reteaching.

02

Reading Intervention Map

Support phonemic awareness, decoding, fluency, comprehension, vocabulary, handwriting, and oral language through small-group reteaching.

03

Math Strategy Map

Teach with objects, drawings, ten-frames, number lines, equations, place value tools, measurement, graphs, clocks, and explanations.

04

Observation Checklist

Track decoding, fluency, writing, math reasoning, inquiry, SEL, movement, creativity, independence, and portfolio evidence.

05

Portfolio Rubric

Collect weekly evidence: writing sample, math sheet, inquiry drawing, oral-language note, project record, assessment score, and teacher comments.

06

Printable Workshop Guide

Use the centered worksheets for calm practice. They support instruction and hands-on learning.

07

Family Communication

Send a weekly home note with essential question, phonics focus, math focus, read-aloud idea, family observation, and celebration.

08

80% Certification Guide

Certificate unlocks only when weekly average, midterm, final, and portfolio evidence all meet the completion gates.
