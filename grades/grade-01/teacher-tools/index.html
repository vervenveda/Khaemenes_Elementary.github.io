Mastery policy

Weekly quiz mastery is 80%. Students may try again. Save best score. Use adult observation and portfolio evidence for first-grade performance tasks.

Daily rhythm

Warm-up → direct instruction → guided practice → workshop → reflection → evidence. Keep work calm, concrete, and joyful.
