# Khaemenes Academy Second Grade — 36 Week Subject Academy

Second Grade is the Grade 02 course inside Khaemenes Elementary.

## Curriculum

- 36 weeks
- 8 subject halls
- 5 lesson blocks per subject per week
- 1,440 subject lesson blocks
- 36 printable packets
- 36 weekly mastery assessments
- midterm
- final
- teacher / family verification tools
- portfolio evidence
- 80% certification

## Unified Academy formula

```text
Academy Family Registry
        ↓
Elementary continuity
        ↓
NAIB mentor routing
        ↓
Archaemenes · Young Scholar
        ↓
Grade 02 course context
        ↓
Assessment evidence
        ↓
Adult verification
        ↓
Learner-scoped Grade 02 record / certificate
```

### Identity

The Academy Family Registry is authoritative for learner identity. Grade 02 does not create a second learner account.

Formal Grade 02 records require the active Academy learner to be assigned to `grade-02`.

### Mentor

Archaemenes is the current Elementary mentor. Grade 02 does not define local alternate mentors or custom mentor authority. NAIB routes the mentor; the page only presents the approved assignment.

### Mastery

The student landing page does not self-award mastery. Weekly results, midterm, final, and portfolio approval are recorded from the adult Teacher / Family Tools surface after assessment evidence is reviewed.

The certificate requires:

- 36/36 weeks at 80% or higher;
- midterm at 80% or higher;
- final at 80% or higher;
- portfolio evidence approved;
- active eligible Grade 02 Academy learner.

### Record migration

The older shared browser record key is retained only as a compatibility source. `assets/khaemenes-grade2-continuity.js` performs a non-destructive copy into learner-scoped records when an eligible Grade 02 learner is active.

## Shared Academy dependencies

This public static course uses first-party Khaemenes Academy continuity resources for Family Registry and mentor routing. It does not require third-party scripts, fonts, trackers, advertising, or curriculum services.
