Weeks 01–06 · Learning Across Subjects

Supplementary guided practice for Grade 2. The existing subject halls, quizzes, 80% mastery requirements, portfolio, and learner records remain authoritative; this companion saves no scores or identities.

Week 01 · Reading carefully

Literacy: Read a short story twice and locate two details that explain the character’s decision.

Mathematics: Build and explain two-digit numbers with tens and ones.

Inquiry and community: Observe a local plant or photo, separate observations from assumptions.

Guide: Model once; ask the learner to explain with words, pictures, or manipulatives. Challenge: compare two strategies and defend a choice.

Week 02 · Patterns and community

Literacy: Recognize beginning, middle, and end; change one event and predict its consequence.

Mathematics: Build a repeated pattern and a growing numerical pattern; justify the next two terms.

Inquiry and community: Describe respectful teamwork in a fictional community.

Guide: Model once; ask the learner to explain with words, pictures, or manipulatives. Challenge: compare two strategies and defend a choice.

Week 03 · Adding and checking

Literacy: Identify a main idea and two supporting details in a short nonfiction paragraph.

Mathematics: Solve addition within 100 using place value and verify by a different method.

Inquiry and community: Record a simple two-column tally and explain what it shows.

Guide: Model once; ask the learner to explain with words, pictures, or manipulatives. Challenge: compare two strategies and defend a choice.

Week 04 · Subtracting and revising

Literacy: Draft three connected sentences, then revise one for clarity.

Mathematics: Use an open number line and place-value blocks to solve subtraction within 100.

Inquiry and community: Test and revise a safe design using recycled paper or blocks.

Guide: Model once; ask the learner to explain with words, pictures, or manipulatives. Challenge: compare two strategies and defend a choice.

Week 05 · Measuring and describing

Literacy: Write a precise description of a familiar object with sensory details.

Mathematics: Compare lengths with a ruler using consistent units, and explain the difference.

Inquiry and community: Repeat a measurement and discuss sources of error.

Guide: Model once; ask the learner to explain with words, pictures, or manipulatives. Challenge: compare two strategies and defend a choice.

Week 06 · Integrating and reflecting

Literacy: Compare two short sources, identify agreement and one unanswered question.

Mathematics: Create a two-step word problem and show two representations of its solution.

Inquiry and community: Make a one-page mini-report with a question, evidence, conclusion, and next step.

Guide: Model once; ask the learner to explain with words, pictures, or manipulatives. Challenge: compare two strategies and defend a choice.
