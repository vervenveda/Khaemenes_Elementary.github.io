Weeks 07–12 · Compare, Investigate, Explain

Optional instructional extension. Keep using existing subject halls, saved work, mastery assessments and learner records. This companion collects no scores or identity data.

Week 07 · Comparing ideas

Literacy: Read two short descriptions of one event; cite where they agree and differ.

Mathematics: Explain addition within 100 with tens, ones and an open number line.

Inquiry: Compare two observations of the same object and name a source of uncertainty.

Support: Model once, then offer spoken, illustrated and written explanations. Stretch: Ask for a different method with evidence.

Week 08 · Working with information

Literacy: Identify headings, captions, main idea and supporting evidence in a nonfiction passage.

Mathematics: Gather nonpersonal class data and build a picture or bar graph with labels.

Inquiry: Tell what a chart shows and one question it cannot answer.

Support: Model once, then offer spoken, illustrated and written explanations. Stretch: Ask for a different method with evidence.

Week 09 · Shapes and structures

Literacy: Use accurate descriptive words for shapes and arrangements.

Mathematics: Compose and partition rectangles into equal shares using cut paper.

Inquiry: Build a safe paper structure and explain how shape affects stability.

Support: Model once, then offer spoken, illustrated and written explanations. Stretch: Ask for a different method with evidence.

Week 10 · Time and change

Literacy: Write a clear chronological sequence using first, next and finally.

Mathematics: Read an analog clock to five-minute intervals with guided practice.

Inquiry: Record changes in a shadow or weather observation and avoid unsupported conclusions.

Support: Model once, then offer spoken, illustrated and written explanations. Stretch: Ask for a different method with evidence.

Week 11 · Solving problems together

Literacy: Compare two fictional perspectives and write a respectful compromise.

Mathematics: Create and solve a two-step addition or subtraction word problem.

Inquiry: Test a cooperative plan, document what worked and revise it.

Support: Model once, then offer spoken, illustrated and written explanations. Stretch: Ask for a different method with evidence.

Week 12 · Explain and revise

Literacy: Write a paragraph with a claim, two details and a closing sentence.

Mathematics: Check a solution using another representation and explain any discrepancy.

Inquiry: Prepare an evidence poster with a question, observation and limitation.

Support: Model once, then offer spoken, illustrated and written explanations. Stretch: Ask for a different method with evidence.
