Weeks 13–18 · Reason, Measure, Reflect

Optional guided practice alongside existing subject halls. Formal assessments, student data and mastery records remain independent of this guide.

Week 13 · Place value and explanations

Literacy: Read a short nonfiction text; identify the main idea and evidence.

Mathematics: Represent three-digit numbers with hundreds, tens and ones; compare two models.

Inquiry: Record an observation and distinguish a measurement from an estimate.

Support: Show one worked example and offer pictures, counters or sentence starters. Extension: Explain another method and what evidence supports it.

Week 14 · Using evidence to compare

Literacy: Compare two descriptions of one event and cite details from each.

Mathematics: Solve addition and subtraction within 100 with a written explanation.

Inquiry: Make a simple table of repeated safe observations and identify a pattern.

Support: Show one worked example and offer pictures, counters or sentence starters. Extension: Explain another method and what evidence supports it.

Week 15 · Fractions in daily life

Literacy: Describe equal sharing in a brief, clearly sequenced explanation.

Mathematics: Partition shapes into halves, thirds and fourths; explain why shares must be equal.

Inquiry: Compare models of equal sharing in fictional community scenarios.

Support: Show one worked example and offer pictures, counters or sentence starters. Extension: Explain another method and what evidence supports it.

Week 16 · Measurement and investigation

Literacy: Read and follow a safe three-step procedure; retell its purpose.

Mathematics: Measure lengths with the same unit and compare values; check with a second measurement.

Inquiry: Ask what could make measurements differ and record results.

Support: Show one worked example and offer pictures, counters or sentence starters. Extension: Explain another method and what evidence supports it.

Week 17 · Maps and community

Literacy: Write an informative paragraph about a fictional neighborhood using spatial words.

Mathematics: Create a labeled grid map and reason about distances and paths.

Inquiry: Discuss accessibility and public spaces through invented examples.

Support: Show one worked example and offer pictures, counters or sentence starters. Extension: Explain another method and what evidence supports it.

Week 18 · Midyear synthesis

Literacy: Revise a paragraph for stronger evidence and clearer sequence.

Mathematics: Create, solve and verify a multi-step word problem using two representations.

Inquiry: Present a mini-investigation with a question, method, observations and limitation.

Support: Show one worked example and offer pictures, counters or sentence starters. Extension: Explain another method and what evidence supports it.
