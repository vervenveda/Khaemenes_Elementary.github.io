Weeks 25–30 · Design, Investigate, Explain

Supplementary offline-ready guided practice. Existing Grade 2 subject halls, student progress, assessments, and certificates remain authoritative.

Week 25 · Reading claims and evidence

Language Arts: Distinguish an author's claim from a supporting fact.

Mathematics: Use place-value strategies to explain addition and subtraction within 1,000.

Inquiry: Compare two safe observations and describe what the evidence supports.

Adaptations: Allow manipulatives, pictures, spoken explanation or independent writing. Extend with a second strategy and a question about evidence.

Week 26 · Graphs and questions

Language Arts: Summarize a chart in two complete sentences with precise labels.

Mathematics: Construct a bar graph from nonpersonal observations and solve comparison questions.

Inquiry: Identify a question the data cannot answer.

Adaptations: Allow manipulatives, pictures, spoken explanation or independent writing. Extend with a second strategy and a question about evidence.

Week 27 · Geometry and design

Language Arts: Describe a structure with accurate shape and location vocabulary.

Mathematics: Partition shapes into equal parts and explain congruent or unequal shares.

Inquiry: Design a paper bridge, test it safely and record revisions.

Adaptations: Allow manipulatives, pictures, spoken explanation or independent writing. Extend with a second strategy and a question about evidence.

Week 28 · Measuring change

Language Arts: Write clear directions for a reproducible measurement.

Mathematics: Measure length in standard units and compare results.

Inquiry: Repeat an observation and discuss a source of uncertainty.

Language Arts: Compare viewpoints in a fictional civic scenario fairly.

Mathematics: Model a budget with addition, subtraction and amounts of money.

Inquiry: Propose a community improvement using a reason and evidence.

Week 30 · Independent investigation

Language Arts: Draft and revise a paragraph with a claim, evidence and conclusion.

Mathematics: Invent a multi-step practical problem, solve and check two ways.

Inquiry: Present an inquiry question, method, result and limitation.
