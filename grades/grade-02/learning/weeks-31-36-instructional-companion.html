Weeks 31–36 · Apply, Reflect, Advance

Optional offline-friendly instruction. Formal assessment scores, mastery, learner records and certificates remain in the existing subject-based curriculum.

Week 31 · Synthesize across sources

Language Arts: Compare two short informational accounts and cite agreement and differences.

Mathematics: Explain a three-digit addition strategy using place-value diagrams.

Inquiry: Describe why a conclusion needs observations.

Support: Model a first example; allow spoken, drawn, or written explanations. Extend: Compare a second strategy, identify a limitation, or revise the result.
