Arts & Music · Week 05

Subtraction Within 100

movement phrase and beat patterns

Evidence Studio · Week 05

Learn: A repeating rhythm can be written as clap, clap, rest, then repeated. A pattern must have a predictable unit.

Create and explain: Create a four-beat rhythm with claps and pauses; perform it twice and draw its symbol sequence.

Reflection: Identify a revision or new question; this activity does not modify the formal mastery record.

Monday: Visual Art: movement phrase and beat patterns

Objective: Students build visual art skill through movement phrase and beat patterns within the weekly theme Subtraction Within 100.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for movement phrase and beat patterns.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.
