Arts & Music · Week 07

Plant Life Cycles and Data

nature prints and rhythm

Monday: Visual Art: nature prints and rhythm

Objective: Students build visual art skill through nature prints and rhythm within the weekly theme Plant Life Cycles and Data.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for nature prints and rhythm.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing visual art progress.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1 — Model and vocabulary: Illustrate four seed-to-plant stages, then compose a four-beat rhythm whose pattern repeats with one intentional change. Identify the goal, define two essential terms and model one concrete step.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Assessment-aligned guided teaching · Day 1: Observe a fictional leaf imprint and distinguish the textured print from a drawing of the leaf.

Week 07 Applied Worked Studio · Day 1: Draw a safe paper rubbing of a leaf and label shape and veins.

Evidence criteria: Show a labeled model or safe demonstration, explain one decision, and correct one error before presenting. This is formative practice, not an official score.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 2 — Guided evidence practice: Illustrate four seed-to-plant stages, then compose a four-beat rhythm whose pattern repeats with one intentional change. Construct a labeled example; check units, symbols or safety choices with a partner.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Assessment-aligned guided teaching · Day 2: Model a safe nature print using a pre-collected clean leaf or a paper rubbing substitute; label shape and vein details.

Week 07 Applied Worked Studio · Day 2: Write a four-beat score: clap, clap, rest, tap; explain that a rest is silent.

Evidence criteria: Show a labeled model or safe demonstration, explain one decision, and correct one error before presenting. This is formative practice, not an official score.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 3 — Independent creation: Illustrate four seed-to-plant stages, then compose a four-beat rhythm whose pattern repeats with one intentional change. Create an original version with a recorded observation, calculation or design decision.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Assessment-aligned guided teaching · Day 5: Exhibit the revised nature print beside a readable four-beat score and explain two artistic decisions.

Week 07 Applied Worked Studio · Day 5: Present labeled nature art and a readable four-beat performance score with one revision.
