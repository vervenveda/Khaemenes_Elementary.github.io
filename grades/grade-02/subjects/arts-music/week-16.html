Arts & Music · Week 16

Research Skills and Sources

museum labels and exhibit design

Monday: Visual Art: museum labels and exhibit design

Objective: Students build visual art skill through museum labels and exhibit design within the weekly theme Research Skills and Sources.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for museum labels and exhibit design.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing visual art progress.
