Arts & Music · Week 25

Character, Citizenship, and Persuasion

public art and voice

Monday: Visual Art: public art and voice

Objective: Students build visual art skill through public art and voice within the weekly theme Character, Citizenship, and Persuasion.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for public art and voice.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing visual art progress.
