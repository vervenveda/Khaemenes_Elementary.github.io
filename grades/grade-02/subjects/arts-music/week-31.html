Arts & Music · Week 31

Literature Circles and Theater

theatre production

Monday: Visual Art: theatre production

Objective: Students build visual art skill through theatre production within the weekly theme Literature Circles and Theater.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for theatre production.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing visual art progress.
