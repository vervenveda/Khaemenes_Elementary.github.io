Arts & Music · Week 35

Second Grade Capstone

capstone performance/exhibit

Monday: Visual Art: capstone performance/exhibit

Objective: Students build visual art skill through capstone performance/exhibit within the weekly theme Second Grade Capstone.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for capstone performance/exhibit.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing visual art progress.
