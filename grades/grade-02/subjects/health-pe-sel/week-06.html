Evidence Studio · Week 06

Focus: Inclusive movement plans.

Study: Compare walking, rolling and seated reaches; distinguish a safe choice from pressure to participate.

Worked model: Teacher models a two-column choice chart: movement option and safety adaptation; include water and rest.

Independent practice: Learners design three optional stations and explain how each serves the same movement goal.

Challenge and revision: Critique a plan that requires everyone to stand; revise for access, consent and safe spacing.

Evidence and reflection: Create a five-minute fictional routine with two alternative movements, a stop signal and reflection. This is optional instructional evidence; formal assessments, scores and mastery remain separate.

Monday: Health: healthy habits and hygiene

Objective: Students build health skill through healthy habits and hygiene within the weekly theme Informational Text and Measurement.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for healthy habits and hygiene.

Teacher script: Introduce Health in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing health progress.

Depth Lab · Day 1: Define inclusive participation and identify walking, rolling, or seated reaching as valid movement options.

Reasoning check: Show the evidence behind your decision, identify a likely error and revise your work. This is practice; established scores and mastery remain separate.
