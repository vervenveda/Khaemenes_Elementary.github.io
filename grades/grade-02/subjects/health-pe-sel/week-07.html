Health, PE & SEL · Week 07

Plant Life Cycles and Data

nutrition and choices

Monday: Health: nutrition and choices

Objective: Students build health skill through nutrition and choices within the weekly theme Plant Life Cycles and Data.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for nutrition and choices.

Teacher script: Introduce Health in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing health progress.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1 — Model and vocabulary: Create a safe garden movement game with walking, rolling or seated gestures; practice requests for rest and hydration. Identify the goal, define two essential terms and model one concrete step.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Assessment-aligned guided teaching · Day 1: Classify fictional meals by variety: a fruit, vegetable, grain and protein; describe why balance matters without labeling foods 'bad'.

Week 07 Applied Worked Studio · Day 1: Describe food variety using a fictional balanced meal and avoid ranking people or foods.

Evidence criteria: Show a labeled model or safe demonstration, explain one decision, and correct one error before presenting. This is formative practice, not an official score.
