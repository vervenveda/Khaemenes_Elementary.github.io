Health, PE & SEL · Week 16

Research Skills and Sources

respectful questions

Monday: Health: respectful questions

Objective: Students build health skill through respectful questions within the weekly theme Research Skills and Sources.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for respectful questions.

Teacher script: Introduce Health in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing health progress.
