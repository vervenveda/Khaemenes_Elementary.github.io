Health, PE & SEL · Week 34

Research Project Workshop

presentation confidence

Monday: Health: presentation confidence

Objective: Students build health skill through presentation confidence within the weekly theme Research Project Workshop.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for presentation confidence.

Teacher script: Introduce Health in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing health progress.
