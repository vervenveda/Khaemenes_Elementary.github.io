helper interview card

Evidence Studio · Week 05

Learn: A class project combines reading, numbers and observation. In a fictional materials survey, measured counts differ from preferences.

Practice: Build a three-category picture tally and explain one observation and one question.

Reflect: Explain one improvement. Assessment systems remain unchanged.

Monday: Research: helper interview card

Objective: Students build research skill through helper interview card within the weekly theme Subtraction Within 100.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for helper interview card.

Teacher script: Introduce Research in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing research progress.
