Evidence Studio · Week 06

Essential question: How can measurements and careful observations support a trustworthy informational report?

Study and vocabulary: Observation is what you notice or measure; inference is a possible explanation. A centimeter is a unit of length. A measurement should begin at the ruler's zero mark, not its physical edge. A source note identifies where the plant or image came from; a limitation describes what the evidence cannot show.

Worked model: A fictional seedling measures 6 cm from the soil line to its highest point on Monday and 9 cm on Friday. Subtract to find growth: 9 − 6 = 3 cm. Write: “The seedling was 3 cm taller on Friday than on Monday.” Do not claim the extra height proves which factor caused growth: light, water and other conditions were not controlled. Draw the stem and two visible leaves; label stem, leaf and soil line.

Guided reasoning: A partner writes “It grew 9 cm.” Explain why that confuses final height with change. Correct the sentence and check that both measurements use cm. Discuss why a drawing made from memory is less reliable than an observation recorded at the time.

Independent investigation: Observe a safe household plant or use this fictional data: Day 1 = 4 cm, Day 3 = 5 cm, Day 5 = 7 cm. Create a dated three-row table, a labeled drawing, and three sentences separating observation from inference. Calculate Day 1 to Day 5 change. Note whether the data are real or fictional. Never taste plants or collect unknown specimens.

Challenge: Two students measured the same plant at 7 cm and 8 cm. Propose two ways differing measurement methods could explain the result and design a repeatable measuring procedure. Explain why a single observation cannot prove a general rule.

Evidence and reflection: Present your labeled drawing, table, calculation (7 − 4 = 3 cm for the fictional challenge data), source note, limitation and one revision. A teacher may hear an oral explanation. This studio supplies optional academic evidence; graded assessments and saved results remain separate.
