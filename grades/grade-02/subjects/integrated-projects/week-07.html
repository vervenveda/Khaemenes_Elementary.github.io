Integrated Projects · Week 07

Plant Life Cycles and Data

garden data report

Monday: Research: garden data report

Objective: Students build research skill through garden data report within the weekly theme Plant Life Cycles and Data.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for garden data report.

Teacher script: Introduce Research in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing research progress.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1 — Model and vocabulary: Produce a plant-growth exhibit with a dated 2, 4, 5 cm sample series, diagram and evidence caveat. Identify the goal, define two essential terms and model one concrete step.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.
