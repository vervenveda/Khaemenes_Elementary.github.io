Technology, Coding, and Communication

unplugged coding maze

Monday: Research: unplugged coding maze

Objective: Students build research skill through unplugged coding maze within the weekly theme Technology, Coding, and Communication.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for unplugged coding maze.

Teacher script: Introduce Research in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing research progress.
