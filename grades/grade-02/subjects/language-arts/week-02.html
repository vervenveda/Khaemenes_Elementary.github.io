Language Arts · Week 02

Long Vowels, Number Fluency, and Careful Observation

long vowels and vowel teams

Worked Example · Evidence Practice

Study: Original text: 'Jade made a paper boat. She set it beside a stone. At home, she wrote a note about her boat.' Long a appears in made and Jade; oa appears in boat.

Try independently: Underline words with long vowel patterns. Retell the sequence using first, next and finally.

Learning evidence: Explain a choice, note an uncertainty, and revise a mistake. Formal assessment and progress records remain separate.

Monday: Reading: long vowels and vowel teams

Objective: Students build reading skill through long vowels and vowel teams within the weekly theme Long Vowels, Number Fluency, and Careful Observation.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for long vowels and vowel teams.

Teacher script: Introduce Reading in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing reading progress.
