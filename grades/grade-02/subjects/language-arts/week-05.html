Language Arts · Week 05

Subtraction Within 100

decodable multisyllable words

Monday: Reading: decodable multisyllable words

Objective: Students build reading skill through decodable multisyllable words within the weekly theme Subtraction Within 100.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for decodable multisyllable words.

Teacher script: Introduce Reading in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing reading progress.
