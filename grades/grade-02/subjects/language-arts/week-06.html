Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: Original passage: A seed needs water and air to grow. Roots take in water while leaves capture light.

Independent practice: Underline two sentences that explain the main topic.

Reflect: Explain one decision, correct an error, and retain optional work. Existing graded assessments are unchanged.

Monday: Reading: main topic and key details

Objective: Students build reading skill through main topic and key details within the weekly theme Informational Text and Measurement.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for main topic and key details.

Teacher script: Introduce Reading in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing reading progress.

Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Read: 'Roots absorb water. Leaves receive sunlight. Stems support leaves.' Name the main topic and cite two details.

Explain and revise: Show a drawing, calculation, quotation or observation supporting your reasoning; find a likely misconception and correct it. This is instructional practice, not a replacement assessment.
