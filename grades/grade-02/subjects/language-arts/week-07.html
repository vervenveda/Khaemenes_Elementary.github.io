Language Arts · Week 07

Plant Life Cycles and Data

research captions and glossary words

Monday: Reading: research captions and glossary words

Objective: Students build reading skill through research captions and glossary words within the weekly theme Plant Life Cycles and Data.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for research captions and glossary words.

Teacher script: Introduce Reading in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing reading progress.

Week 07 Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Read an original three-sentence seed-to-plant account; identify its topic and two supporting details.

Evidence and revision: Support your explanation with an observation, quotation, diagram or calculation; identify one possible error and improve your response. This practice does not award formal mastery.
