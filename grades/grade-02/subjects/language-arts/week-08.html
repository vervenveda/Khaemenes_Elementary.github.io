Language Arts · Week 08

Fables, Folktales, and Word Problems

fables, morals, and retelling

Monday: Reading: fables, morals, and retelling

Objective: Students build reading skill through fables, morals, and retelling within the weekly theme Fables, Folktales, and Word Problems.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for fables, morals, and retelling.

Teacher script: Introduce Reading in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing reading progress.
