Language Arts · Week 20

Author Study and Addition to 1,000

author craft and series study

Monday: Reading: author craft and series study

Objective: Students build reading skill through author craft and series study within the weekly theme Author Study and Addition to 1,000.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for author craft and series study.

Teacher script: Introduce Reading in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing reading progress.
