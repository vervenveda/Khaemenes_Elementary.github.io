Language Arts · Week 33

Technology, Coding, and Communication

how-to writing and sequence language

Monday: Reading: how-to writing and sequence language

Objective: Students build reading skill through how-to writing and sequence language within the weekly theme Technology, Coding, and Communication.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for how-to writing and sequence language.

Teacher script: Introduce Reading in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing reading progress.
