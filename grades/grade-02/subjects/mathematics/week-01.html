Mathematics · Week 01

Scholar Routines and Reading Stamina

number habits and strategy talk

Worked Example · Apply and Explain

Study: Use counters to model 8 + 5. Start at eight and count on five: 9, 10, 11, 12, 13. Check by arranging eight and five objects into a combined group.

Independent practice: Draw two ways to make 13 and write a sentence explaining how both methods show the same total.

Check understanding: Explain one step, note one correction or question, and keep the work as optional lesson evidence. Formal mastery is assessed separately.

Worked Example and Independent Practice

Study: Model 8 + 5 by starting at eight and counting on: 9, 10, 11, 12, 13. Check by combining eight and five counters.

Practice: Draw two ways to make 13 and explain why both show the same total.

Reflect: Explain one step or revise an error. Existing graded assessments remain separate.

Monday: Operations: number habits and strategy talk

Objective: Students build operations skill through number habits and strategy talk within the weekly theme Scholar Routines and Reading Stamina.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for number habits and strategy talk.

Teacher script: Introduce Operations in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing operations progress.
