Mathematics · Week 06

Informational Text and Measurement

standard units of length

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: A 34 cm ribbon is 6 cm longer than a 28 cm ribbon because 34 − 28 = 6.

Independent practice: Draw a ruler and label the difference in centimeters.

Reflect: Explain one decision, correct an error, and retain optional work. Existing graded assessments are unchanged.

Monday: Operations: standard units of length

Objective: Students build operations skill through standard units of length within the weekly theme Informational Text and Measurement.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for standard units of length.

Teacher script: Introduce Operations in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing operations progress.

Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Model 34 cm and 28 cm on a number line; calculate the difference of 6 cm and label units.

Explain and revise: Show a drawing, calculation, quotation or observation supporting your reasoning; find a likely misconception and correct it. This is instructional practice, not a replacement assessment.
