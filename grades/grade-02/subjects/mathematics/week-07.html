Mathematics · Week 07

Plant Life Cycles and Data

data tables and picture graphs

Monday: Operations: data tables and picture graphs

Objective: Students build operations skill through data tables and picture graphs within the weekly theme Plant Life Cycles and Data.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for data tables and picture graphs.

Teacher script: Introduce Operations in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing operations progress.

Week 07 Mathematics Depth Lab · Day 1: Model a fictional seedling count: Monday 4, Tuesday 6, Wednesday 8. Read the table aloud and explain that each number counts seedlings, not centimeters.

Proof and revision: Explain your representation, verify each numerical result, identify a likely misconception and correct it. This is practice, not a formal score.
