Mathematics · Week 13

Poetry, Fractions, and Rhythm

halves, thirds, and fourths

Monday: Operations: halves, thirds, and fourths

Objective: Students build operations skill through halves, thirds, and fourths within the weekly theme Poetry, Fractions, and Rhythm.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for halves, thirds, and fourths.

Teacher script: Introduce Operations in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing operations progress.
