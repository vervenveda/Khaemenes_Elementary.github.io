Geometry and Design

2D/3D shapes and attributes

Monday: Operations: 2D/3D shapes and attributes

Objective: Students build operations skill through 2D/3D shapes and attributes within the weekly theme Geometry and Design.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for 2D/3D shapes and attributes.

Teacher script: Introduce Operations in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing operations progress.
