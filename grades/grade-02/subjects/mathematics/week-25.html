Mathematics · Week 25

Character, Citizenship, and Persuasion

multi-step word problems

Monday: Operations: multi-step word problems

Objective: Students build operations skill through multi-step word problems within the weekly theme Character, Citizenship, and Persuasion.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for multi-step word problems.

Teacher script: Introduce Operations in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing operations progress.
