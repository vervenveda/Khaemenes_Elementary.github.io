plant needs and observations

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: A plant experiment comparing water amounts needs matched seeds, pots and light.

Independent practice: Create an invented three-day observation table and name a controlled variable.

Reflect: Explain one decision, correct an error, and retain optional work. Existing graded assessments are unchanged.

Monday: Observation: plant needs and observations

Objective: Students build observation skill through plant needs and observations within the weekly theme Informational Text and Measurement.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for plant needs and observations.

Teacher script: Introduce Observation in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing observation progress.

Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Define question and fair test; compare two same-type seedlings receiving different water amounts while other conditions match.

Explain and revise: Show a drawing, calculation, quotation or observation supporting your reasoning; find a likely misconception and correct it. This is instructional practice, not a replacement assessment.
