Science · Week 07

Plant Life Cycles and Data

plant life cycles

Monday: Observation: plant life cycles

Objective: Students build observation skill through plant life cycles within the weekly theme Plant Life Cycles and Data.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for plant life cycles.

Teacher script: Introduce Observation in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing observation progress.

Week 07 Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Sequence seed, germination, seedling and mature plant using a labeled cycle diagram.

Evidence and revision: Support your explanation with an observation, quotation, diagram or calculation; identify one possible error and improve your response. This practice does not award formal mastery.
