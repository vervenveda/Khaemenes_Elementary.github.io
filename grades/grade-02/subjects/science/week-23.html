Science · Week 23

Forces, Motion, and Fair Tests

pushes, pulls, motion, and fair tests

Monday: Observation: pushes, pulls, motion, and fair tests

Objective: Students build observation skill through pushes, pulls, motion, and fair tests within the weekly theme Forces, Motion, and Fair Tests.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for pushes, pulls, motion, and fair tests.

Teacher script: Introduce Observation in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing observation progress.
