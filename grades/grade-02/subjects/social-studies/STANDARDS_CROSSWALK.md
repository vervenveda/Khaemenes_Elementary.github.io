# Grade 2 Social Studies · National Portability Crosswalk

The course intentionally exceeds a single-state checklist.

## NCSS C3
Questions/inquiry, disciplinary tools, source/evidence work, conclusions, and informed action are embedded throughout.
https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3

## Florida Grade 2
Coverage includes primary/secondary sources; Indigenous history and regional comparison; colonial America; immigration then/now and contributions; African American oral traditions and public-service contributions; government, Constitution, citizenship and civic symbols/documents; supply/demand, saving/spending and international trade; and expanded North American map skills.
https://www.fldoe.org/academics/standards/subject-areas/social-studies/

## California Grade 2
Coverage includes family/community history through sources; chronology and change; grid/location systems and essential map elements; North American geography; urban/suburban/rural land use; governmental institutions and how laws are made/carried out/reviewed; people who make a difference; and basic economic reasoning.
https://www.cde.ca.gov/ci/hs/

## Assessment integrity
Formal Social Studies progression requires 80% on every weekly subject gate, the closed-book midterm, and the closed-book cumulative final. The whole-grade eight-subject checklist remains separate portfolio evidence.

## Inclusion and privacy
No learner must disclose immigration/citizenship status, street address, family finances, political affiliation, religion, or other sensitive personal information. Fictional/public examples are valid.

## State/local localization

State localization is required across the Khaemenes Social Studies sequence. Each grade keeps a broad national disciplinary core while adapting appropriate examples, sources, maps, institutions, Indigenous histories, biographies, civic cases, and community evidence to the learner's state/locality. Localization does not lower the 80% mastery threshold or delete national competencies.

See `STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`.
