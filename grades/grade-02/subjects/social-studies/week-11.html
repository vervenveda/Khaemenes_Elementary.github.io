Time and Schedules

daily life across communities

Monday: Civics: daily life across communities

Objective: Students build civics skill through daily life across communities within the weekly theme Time and Schedules.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for daily life across communities.

Teacher script: Introduce Civics in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing civics progress.
