Social Studies · Week 17

Midyear Review

social studies review inquiry

Monday: Civics: social studies review inquiry

Objective: Students build civics skill through social studies review inquiry within the weekly theme Midyear Review.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for social studies review inquiry.

Teacher script: Introduce Civics in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing civics progress.
