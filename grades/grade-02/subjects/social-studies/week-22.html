Informational Writing Lab

community resources and economics

Monday: Civics: community resources and economics

Objective: Students build civics skill through community resources and economics within the weekly theme Informational Writing Lab.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for community resources and economics.

Teacher script: Introduce Civics in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing civics progress.
