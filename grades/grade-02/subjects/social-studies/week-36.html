Final Review and Celebration

final social studies review

Monday: Civics: final social studies review

Objective: Students build civics skill through final social studies review within the weekly theme Final Review and Celebration.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for final social studies review.

Teacher script: Introduce Civics in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing civics progress.
