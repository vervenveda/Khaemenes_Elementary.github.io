Evidence Studio · Week 06

Focus: Plant measurement tables.

Study: Distinguish dates, heights and units in an observation table; explain why blank entries cannot be guessed.

Worked model: Using fictional heights of 4 cm, 5 cm and 7 cm, show that growth from first to last measurement is 3 cm.

Independent practice: Create a four-row table with date, measured height, unit and source; mark one entry unknown.

Challenge and revision: Correct a table that mixes mm and cm without conversion; state what information is missing.

Evidence and reflection: Present a labeled table and one cautious conclusion, separating observed growth from its possible causes. This is optional instructional evidence; formal assessments, scores and mastery remain separate.

Monday: Digital Citizenship: digital citizenship: kind communication

Objective: Students build digital citizenship skill through digital citizenship: kind communication within the weekly theme Informational Text and Measurement.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for digital citizenship: kind communication.

Teacher script: Introduce Digital Citizenship in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing digital citizenship progress.

Depth Lab · Day 1: Identify dated observations, cm units and respectful data-sharing rules; do not publish personal information.

Reasoning check: Explain the evidence, identify an error to avoid, and revise your answer. Instructional practice is separate from graded mastery.
