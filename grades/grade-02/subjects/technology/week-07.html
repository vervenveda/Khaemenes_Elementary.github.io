organize files and folders conceptually

Monday: Digital Citizenship: organize files and folders conceptually

Objective: Students build digital citizenship skill through organize files and folders conceptually within the weekly theme Plant Life Cycles and Data.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for organize files and folders conceptually.

Teacher script: Introduce Digital Citizenship in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing digital citizenship progress.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1 — Model and vocabulary: Create a four-day fictional plant-growth table with dates and centimeter units; inspect a missing entry before graphing. Identify the goal, define two essential terms and model one concrete step.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Assessment-aligned guided teaching · Day 1: Model a folder tree: Schoolwork > Science > Plant Observation; define file, folder and meaningful filename.

Week 07 Applied Worked Studio · Day 1: Draw folders Schoolwork > Science > Plant Study with one labeled file inside.

Evidence criteria: Show a labeled model or safe demonstration, explain one decision, and correct one error before presenting. This is formative practice, not an official score.
