Monday: Digital Citizenship: source sorting and privacy

Objective: Students build digital citizenship skill through source sorting and privacy within the weekly theme Research Skills and Sources.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for source sorting and privacy.

Teacher script: Introduce Digital Citizenship in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.
