Monday: Digital Citizenship: typing readiness and naming files

Objective: Students build digital citizenship skill through typing readiness and naming files within the weekly theme Reading Fluency and Numbers to 1,000.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for typing readiness and naming files.

Teacher script: Introduce Digital Citizenship in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing digital citizenship progress.
