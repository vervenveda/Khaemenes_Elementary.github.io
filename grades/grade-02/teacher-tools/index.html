Teacher Tools

Second Grade Mentor Toolkit

Academy learner context is loading. Teacher tools advise and document; they do not silently alter formal placement or mastery.

01

Subject Pacing Guide

Use one week at a time, with each subject hall providing five weekly lesson blocks.

02

Reading and Writing Tools

Track decoding, fluency, comprehension, grammar, writing craft, speaking, listening, and research.

03

Math Strategy Tools

Use objects, drawings, equations, number lines, base-ten tools, measurement tools, graphs, clocks, and written explanations.

04

Science/Social Studies Inquiry

Require observation, maps, evidence, question, claim, and student explanation.

05

Portfolio Rubric

Collect weekly evidence from every subject or a balanced rotating set.

06

Printable Workshop Guide

Use the centered worksheets as calm professional practice pages, not a replacement for hands-on work.

07

Intervention/Enrichment

Reteach through smaller steps, manipulatives, oral response, movement, and visuals; extend through projects.

08

80% Certification Guide

Certificate unlocks with weekly average, midterm, final, and portfolio approval.
