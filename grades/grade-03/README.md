# Khaemenes Academy Third Grade — 36 Week A+ Curriculum

Third Grade is a subject-based Elementary course with eight subject halls, integrated weekly plans, printables, assessments, teacher/family tools, portfolio evidence, and learner-scoped certification.

## Curriculum

- 36 weeks
- 8 subject halls
- 1,440 lesson blocks
- weekly integrated plans
- professional printables
- 36 weekly mastery assessments
- midterm and final
- portfolio evidence
- 80% mastery rule

## Unified Academy Architecture

Grade 03 follows the Elementary continuity model:

`Academy Family Registry → Elementary continuity → NAIB → Archaemenes → Grade 03`

The Academy Family Registry owns learner identity. Archaemenes is the current Elementary mentor in Young Scholar presentation. Grade 03 owns its course records, assessment evidence, and certification state.

The landing page does not award mastery. Verified weekly results, midterm, final, and portfolio approval are recorded through Teacher / Family Tools after evidence is reviewed.

Legacy browser records are retained only as a compatibility source and are copied non-destructively into learner-scoped Grade 03 records when an eligible learner is first connected.

## Authority Boundaries

- Identity: Academy Family Registry
- Mentor routing: NAIB
- Mentor: Archaemenes
- Learning and mastery: Grade 03 curriculum / assessments
- Adult verification: Teacher / Family Tools
- Certificate: Grade 03 learner-scoped record
