Weeks 01–06 · Read, Model, Investigate

Optional offline-ready instructional extension. The established eight subject halls, weekly assessments, 80% mastery and student records remain authoritative. This companion stores no grades or learner details.

Week 01 · Read closely, explain clearly

Language Arts: Read a short informational passage, identify its central idea, and quote or paraphrase two supporting details.

Mathematics: Model three-digit place value with diagrams, expanded form, and comparison statements.

Scientific inquiry: Observe one safe natural object and distinguish measurable properties from inferences.

Scaffold: Model one worked example, offer a diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent explanation. Stretch: Ask the learner to compare two strategies and critique the evidence.

Week 02 · Patterns, sequence and cause

Language Arts: Compare the sequence of events in two narratives and explain one cause-and-effect link.

Mathematics: Extend numeric patterns with addition and subtraction, then justify a general rule.

Scientific inquiry: Record a repeated observation and explain why one result is insufficient.

Scaffold: Model one worked example, offer a diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent explanation. Stretch: Ask the learner to compare two strategies and critique the evidence.

Week 03 · Multiplication as structure

Language Arts: Write an explanatory paragraph with topic sentence and supporting examples.

Mathematics: Model equal groups and arrays, relate repeated addition to multiplication, and check by skip-counting.

Scientific inquiry: Sort natural or built structures by observable shared characteristics.

Scaffold: Model one worked example, offer a diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent explanation. Stretch: Ask the learner to compare two strategies and critique the evidence.

Week 04 · Division and fair sharing

Language Arts: Summarize a nonfiction article in original words, preserving its key facts.

Mathematics: Represent fair shares and equal groups with counters and multiplication inverses.

Scientific inquiry: Plan a fictional community resource-sharing solution with clear reasoning.

Scaffold: Model one worked example, offer a diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent explanation. Stretch: Ask the learner to compare two strategies and critique the evidence.

Week 05 · Measurement and precision

Language Arts: Write reproducible instructions for an investigation using sequence language.

Mathematics: Measure length and mass with consistent units and compare estimates with observed results.

Scientific inquiry: Repeat a measurement, record variation, and describe plausible sources of error.

Scaffold: Model one worked example, offer a diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent explanation. Stretch: Ask the learner to compare two strategies and critique the evidence.

Week 06 · Evidence-based mini project

Language Arts: Compare two short sources and identify agreement, disagreement, and one question for further inquiry.

Mathematics: Solve a two-step place-value or multiplication problem and verify using another representation.

Scientific inquiry: Create a mini investigation portfolio: question, method, observations, claim, uncertainty and reflection.

Scaffold: Model one worked example, offer a diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent explanation. Stretch: Ask the learner to compare two strategies and critique the evidence.
