Weeks 07–12 · Analyze, Model, Explain

Supplemental offline-ready instruction only. Existing eight subject halls, learner progress, weekly assessments, and mastery requirements remain authoritative.

Week 07 · Interpreting narratives

Language Arts: Compare narrator perspectives using details from two short passages.

Mathematics: Model multiplication with arrays and explain a related division fact.

Inquiry: Compare two observations and distinguish a conclusion from evidence.

Scaffold: Model one worked example, use pictures or manipulatives, and invite oral or written explanation. Extend: Compare methods and defend a claim using evidence.

Week 08 · Organizing information

Language Arts: Read a nonfiction passage and distinguish main ideas from examples.

Mathematics: Interpret scaled picture graphs and solve comparison questions.

Language Arts: Explain equal sharing with precise language and labeled diagrams.

Mathematics: Use fraction strips and number lines to compare unit fractions.

Inquiry: Test which paper folds create equal portions and justify observations.

Scaffold: Model one worked example, use pictures or manipulatives, and invite oral or written explanation. Extend: Compare methods and defend a claim using evidence.
