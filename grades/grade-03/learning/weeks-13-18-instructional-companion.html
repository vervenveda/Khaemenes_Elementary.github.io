Weeks 13–18 · Model, Investigate, Reflect

Supplemental offline-friendly instruction. Official Grade 3 subject lessons, mastery and learner records remain independent.

Week 13 · Multiplication strategies

Language Arts: Analyze two explanations and cite which details support each.

Mathematics: Use area models and distributive reasoning to multiply one-digit factors.

Inquiry: Document repeated observations with a labeled table.

Support: Provide visual models and sentence frames. Stretch: Defend a second solution and identify a limitation.

Week 14 · Division and remainders

Language Arts: Write a coherent explanation of a fair-sharing problem.

Mathematics: Interpret division as grouping and sharing; discuss leftovers in context.

Inquiry: Compare two resource-sharing proposals in a fictional community.

Support: Provide visual models and sentence frames. Stretch: Defend a second solution and identify a limitation.

Week 15 · Fractions on a number line

Language Arts: Summarize an informational text with a main idea and precise supporting details.

Mathematics: Locate unit fractions on a number line and compare with equal-sized wholes.

Inquiry: Test paper-folded fraction models and note why equal parts matter.

Support: Provide visual models and sentence frames. Stretch: Defend a second solution and identify a limitation.
