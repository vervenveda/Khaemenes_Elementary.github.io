Weeks 19–24 · Evidence, Design and Inquiry

Optional offline-friendly practice. Formal learning records, assessment scores, teacher reviews and certificates remain in the existing Grade 3 systems.

Week 19 · Comparing evidence

Language Arts: Compare two accounts and distinguish fact from interpretation.

Mathematics: Model two-digit by one-digit multiplication using arrays and place value.

Inquiry: Record repeated observations and explain uncertainty.

Support: Model one example, provide visual evidence organizers, and accept oral, drawn or written explanations. Extension: Compare methods and critique evidence.

Week 20 · Area and structures

Language Arts: Write a clear procedural explanation with a diagram.

Mathematics: Find area by counting unit squares and relate rectangular arrays to multiplication.

Inquiry: Build and test two paper designs, recording what changes.

Support: Model one example, provide visual evidence organizers, and accept oral, drawn or written explanations. Extension: Compare methods and critique evidence.

Week 21 · Fractions and equivalence

Language Arts: Explain an idea using a claim and two relevant details.

Mathematics: Represent equivalent fractions with equal-sized models and number lines.

Inquiry: Compare repeated paper-folding trials and their limitations.

Support: Model one example, provide visual evidence organizers, and accept oral, drawn or written explanations. Extension: Compare methods and critique evidence.

Week 22 · Plants, habitats and change

Language Arts: Summarize an informational passage and identify its sources of evidence.

Mathematics: Use tables and graphs to compare measured changes.

Inquiry: Observe safe plant photographs over time and distinguish correlation from cause.

Support: Model one example, provide visual evidence organizers, and accept oral, drawn or written explanations. Extension: Compare methods and critique evidence.

Week 23 · Community decisions

Language Arts: Present multiple fictional civic viewpoints respectfully.

Mathematics: Solve multi-step time and money problems with checked calculations.

Inquiry: Design an accessible community improvement and justify it.

Support: Model one example, provide visual evidence organizers, and accept oral, drawn or written explanations. Extension: Compare methods and critique evidence.

Week 24 · Inquiry showcase

Language Arts: Revise a report for clarity and support its claims with evidence.

Mathematics: Create and solve an original multiplication, division or area problem two ways.

Inquiry: Present a question, method, findings, limitations and future investigation.

Support: Model one example, provide visual evidence organizers, and accept oral, drawn or written explanations. Extension: Compare methods and critique evidence.
