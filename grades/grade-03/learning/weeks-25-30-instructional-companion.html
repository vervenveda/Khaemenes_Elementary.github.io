Weeks 25–30 · Research, Design, Explain

Supplementary offline-ready practice. Formal grades, 80% mastery, learner records and assessments remain in the existing Grade 3 curriculum.

Week 25 · Claims and evidence

Literacy: Identify an author's claim and supporting details.

Mathematics: Model multiplication and division with arrays and related facts.

Inquiry: Explain the difference between an observation and an inference.

Support: Model with pictures, manipulatives and sentence frames; accept oral, drawn or written explanations. Extension: Compare two strategies and defend a choice.
