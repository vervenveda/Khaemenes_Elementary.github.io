Weeks 31–36 · Integrate, Demonstrate, Advance

Optional offline instructional practice. The existing eight subject halls, learner records, 80% mastery, weekly assessments and certifications remain authoritative.

Week 31 · Synthesize sources

Language Arts: Compare two nonfiction sources and distinguish agreement, disagreement and missing evidence.

Mathematics: Represent a multi-step multiplication or division problem with arrays and equations.

Scientific inquiry: Form a testable question and describe a fair observation method.

Support: Provide an example, diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent oral, written or drawn evidence. Challenge: Defend an alternative and identify a limitation.

Week 32 · Design and measure

Language Arts: Draft clear instructions and revise ambiguous steps.

Mathematics: Use area, perimeter and standard units to plan a rectangular design.

Scientific inquiry: Build a safe paper prototype and record improvements.

Support: Provide an example, diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent oral, written or drawn evidence. Challenge: Defend an alternative and identify a limitation.

Week 33 · Investigate data

Language Arts: Read a chart critically and write a supported conclusion.

Mathematics: Represent data on a scaled graph, justify a comparison and check totals.

Scientific inquiry: Explain uncertainty and the limitations of a small sample.

Support: Provide an example, diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent oral, written or drawn evidence. Challenge: Defend an alternative and identify a limitation.

Week 34 · Independent problem-solving

Language Arts: Write a short claim–evidence–reasoning explanation.

Mathematics: Solve and check an original multistep word problem using two methods.

Scientific inquiry: Present an investigation's question, controls, observations and caveats.

Support: Provide an example, diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent oral, written or drawn evidence. Challenge: Defend an alternative and identify a limitation.

Week 35 · Reflect and revise

Language Arts: Compare early-year writing with recent work and revise for clarity.

Mathematics: Explain a change in mathematical reasoning using an example.

Scientific inquiry: Select portfolio evidence and identify one next question without peer ranking.

Support: Provide an example, diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent oral, written or drawn evidence. Challenge: Defend an alternative and identify a limitation.

Week 36 · Bridge into Grade 4

Language Arts: Summarize a grade-appropriate information text with supporting evidence.

Mathematics: Combine multiplication, fractions, area and measurement in a practical task.

Scientific inquiry: Present a responsible Grade 4 inquiry proposal and explain how evidence could answer it.

Support: Provide an example, diagram or sentence frame, then invite independent oral, written or drawn evidence. Challenge: Defend an alternative and identify a limitation.
