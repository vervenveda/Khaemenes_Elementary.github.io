Week 05 · Arts & Music

Opinion Writing and Civic Voice

How can a voice be strong, fair, and supported by evidence?

Evidence Studio · Week 05

Learn: An image can communicate an opinion through line, color and placement, but visual choices are not factual proof.

Create and explain: Design a poster for a fictional classroom proposal; label one artistic choice and one factual claim.

Reflection: Identify a revision or new question; this activity does not modify the formal mastery record.

Focus: performance
