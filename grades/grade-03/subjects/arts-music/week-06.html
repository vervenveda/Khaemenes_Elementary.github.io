Week 06 · Arts & Music

Division and Fair Sharing

How do we divide fairly and explain our process?

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Focus: Four-beat rhythm and dramatic expression.

Study: Show how two four-beat phrases make eight beats; explain the difference between sound, rest and expression.

Worked model: Model clap–rest–tap–tap twice and count eight steady beats without rushing the rest.

Independent practice: Compose two four-beat patterns and notate each sound and rest using symbols and words.

Challenge and revision: Compare a repeated pattern with one changed beat; explain which change makes the phrase easier to recognize.

Evidence and reflection: Perform or silently gesture the piece, give a reasoned artistic choice and revise the score. This is optional instructional evidence; formal assessments, scores and mastery remain separate.

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: drama

Objective: Students strengthen arts & music through drama while investigating Division and Fair Sharing.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply drama; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Depth Lab · Day 1: Define phrase, beat and rest; count two groups of four beats to make eight.

Reasoning check: Show the evidence behind your decision, identify a likely error and revise your work. This is practice; established scores and mastery remain separate.
