Week 07 · Arts & Music

Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading

How can diagrams and data explain motion?

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: movement

Objective: Students strengthen arts & music through movement while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply movement; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1 — Model and vocabulary: Make a four-measure sound score representing push, roll, slow and stop; mark rests and explain dynamic choices. Identify the goal, define two essential terms and model one concrete step.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Tuesday: Guided Practice

Focus: cultural connection

Objective: Students strengthen arts & music through cultural connection while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply cultural connection; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 2 — Guided evidence practice: Make a four-measure sound score representing push, roll, slow and stop; mark rests and explain dynamic choices. Construct a labeled example; check units, symbols or safety choices with a partner.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Focus: drawing

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 3 — Independent creation: Make a four-measure sound score representing push, roll, slow and stop; mark rests and explain dynamic choices. Create an original version with a recorded observation, calculation or design decision.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Thursday: Evidence, Revision, and Explanation

Focus: design

Objective: Students strengthen arts & music through design while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 4 — Challenge, error analysis and revision: Make a four-measure sound score representing push, roll, slow and stop; mark rests and explain dynamic choices. Find an ambiguity or incorrect assumption in the model, repair it, and explain why the revision matters.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Focus: critique

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 5 — Presentation and reflection: Make a four-measure sound score representing push, roll, slow and stop; mark rests and explain dynamic choices. Present a revised artifact, two supporting details and one limitation; written, drawn or oral explanation is acceptable.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.
