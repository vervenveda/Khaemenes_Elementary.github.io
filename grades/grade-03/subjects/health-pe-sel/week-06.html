Week 06 · Health, PE & SEL

Division and Fair Sharing

How do we divide fairly and explain our process?

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: Fair teams give everyone a meaningful role, even when the roles are different. A leader invites ideas, checks that tasks are safe, and makes room for classmates with different movement needs. Resilience means adjusting a plan after a mistake rather than blaming a teammate.

Model: Four learners plan a short movement circuit: timekeeper, directions reader, movement demonstrator and encouragement leader. They rotate roles. If someone prefers seated movement, everyone offers an equivalent arm-reach activity; no one is excluded. Afterward, they use feedback such as “Our directions were clear; next time, allow more transition time.”

Independent practice: Sketch a three-station, low-risk movement circuit using available space (for example, arm circles, gentle marching or seated marching, and balance with support). Assign inclusive team roles, identify a water/rest break, and explain how you would adapt one station. No exercise performance or fitness level is required to submit the written plan.

Reflect: Give one specific respectful feedback sentence and revise one step to make participation safer or fairer. Retain the plan as optional evidence; graded mastery stays separate.
