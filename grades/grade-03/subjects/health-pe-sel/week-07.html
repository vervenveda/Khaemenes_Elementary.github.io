Week 07 · Health, PE & SEL

Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading

How can diagrams and data explain motion?

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: resilience

Objective: Students strengthen health, pe & sel through resilience while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply resilience; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1 — Model and vocabulary: Practice safe force-and-motion activities without collisions and compare accessible seated versus standing motions. Identify the goal, define two essential terms and model one concrete step.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.
