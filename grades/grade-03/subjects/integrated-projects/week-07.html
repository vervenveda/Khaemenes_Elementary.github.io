Week 07 · Integrated Projects

Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading

How can diagrams and data explain motion?

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design

Objective: Students strengthen integrated projects through controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply reflection; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1 — Model and vocabulary: Plan a fictional ramp test with a fair-variable table, labeled design diagram and a technical procedure. Identify the goal, define two essential terms and model one concrete step.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Week 07 Project Worked Studio · Day 1 — Worked model: Use a fictional ramp trial table: A = 30 cm, B = 45 cm, C = 40 cm. Mark the release point and label the unit.

Tuesday: Guided Practice

Focus: controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design

Objective: Students strengthen integrated projects through controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply inquiry questions; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 2 — Guided evidence practice: Plan a fictional ramp test with a fair-variable table, labeled design diagram and a technical procedure. Construct a labeled example; check units, symbols or safety choices with a partner.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Week 07 Project Worked Studio · Day 2 — Guided procedure: Write a four-step test holding car, surface and starting position constant; identify what may change.

Wednesday: Workshop and Application

Focus: controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design

Objective: Students strengthen integrated projects through controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply planning; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 3 — Independent creation: Plan a fictional ramp test with a fair-variable table, labeled design diagram and a technical procedure. Create an original version with a recorded observation, calculation or design decision.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Week 07 Project Worked Studio · Day 3 — Independent design: Draw a ramp, record three sample distances and graph them on equally spaced centimeter intervals.

Thursday: Evidence, Revision, and Explanation

Focus: controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design

Objective: Students strengthen integrated projects through controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply evidence; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 4 — Challenge, error analysis and revision: Plan a fictional ramp test with a fair-variable table, labeled design diagram and a technical procedure. Find an ambiguity or incorrect assumption in the model, repair it, and explain why the revision matters.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Week 07 Project Worked Studio · Day 4 — Error analysis: A report says trial B proves the ramp was steeper, but heights were not recorded. Explain why this inference is unsupported.

Friday: Mastery, Reflection, and Portfolio

Focus: controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design

Objective: Students strengthen integrated projects through controlled ramp experiments and evidence-based design while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply calculation; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 5 — Presentation and reflection: Plan a fictional ramp test with a fair-variable table, labeled design diagram and a technical procedure. Present a revised artifact, two supporting details and one limitation; written, drawn or oral explanation is acceptable.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Week 07 Project Worked Studio · Day 5 — Presentation criteria: Submit labeled diagram, measured table, a repeatable procedure, one evidence-based statement and one limitation.
