Week 01 · Language Arts

Third Grade Scholar Launch

How do strong third-grade scholars learn with independence and evidence?

Worked Example · Apply and Explain

Study: Study the word 'unhelpful': un- means not, help is the base, and -ful means full of. A prefix and suffix can change meaning.

Independent practice: Explain 'rewrite' and 'carefully' using their meaningful word parts; then read your sentences aloud.

Check understanding: Explain one step, note one correction or question, and keep the work as optional lesson evidence. Formal mastery is assessed separately.

Worked Example and Independent Practice

Study: In 'unhelpful', un- means not, help is the base and -ful means full of. Changing a word part can change meaning.

Practice: Explain 'rewrite' and 'carefully' by word parts and use each in a sentence.

Reflect: Explain your reasoning and revise one error. Existing assessments stay separate.
