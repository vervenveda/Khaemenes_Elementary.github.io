Week 02 · Language Arts

Text Evidence and Place Value

How do evidence and structure help us explain clearly?

Worked Example · Evidence Practice

Study: Original text: 'Every afternoon, Ravi checked his notebook for notes about the class garden. He compared Monday's rain with Tuesday's dry soil.' One detail supports a comparison, but does not prove its cause.

Try independently: Quote a phrase that gives evidence, then write a separate inference and label it.

Learning evidence: Explain a choice, note an uncertainty, and revise a mistake. Formal assessment and progress records remain separate.
