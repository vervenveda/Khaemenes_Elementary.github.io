Week 06 · Language Arts

Division and Fair Sharing

How do we divide fairly and explain our process?

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: Original passage: Four friends divided 20 library cards equally. Each took five.

Independent practice: Cite the phrase that supports fair sharing and summarize the main idea.

Reflect: Explain a choice, correct a mistake and retain optional evidence. Graded mastery remains separate.

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: writing craft

Objective: Students strengthen language arts through writing craft while investigating Division and Fair Sharing.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply writing craft; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Read: 'Six tables received four pencils each. The class used all 24 pencils.' Identify explicit evidence of equality.

Explain and revise: Show a drawing, calculation, quotation or observation supporting your reasoning; find a likely misconception and correct it. This is instructional practice, not a replacement assessment.

Academic Depth Lab · Day 2: Distinguish main idea from supporting detail; quote 'four pencils each' and explain what it supports.

Explain and revise: Show a drawing, calculation, quotation or observation supporting your reasoning; find a likely misconception and correct it. This is instructional practice, not a replacement assessment.
