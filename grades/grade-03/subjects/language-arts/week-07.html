Week 07 · Language Arts

Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading

How can diagrams and data explain motion?

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: technical instructions, diagrams and precise procedures

Objective: Students strengthen language arts through technical instructions, diagrams and precise procedures while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply research; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Read an original instruction: 'Release the toy car from the same marked ramp point.' Identify action and condition.

Evidence and revision: Support your explanation with an observation, quotation, diagram or calculation; identify one possible error and improve your response. This practice does not award formal mastery.
