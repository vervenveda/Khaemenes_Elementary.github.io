Week 01 · Mathematics

Third Grade Scholar Launch

How do strong third-grade scholars learn with independence and evidence?

Worked Example · Apply and Explain

Study: Division can mean equal groups. For 24 ÷ 6, place 24 counters into 6 equal groups; each group has 4. Check 6 × 4 = 24.

Independent practice: Draw an array for 18 ÷ 3 and explain why the multiplication check proves the quotient.

Check understanding: Explain one step, note one correction or question, and keep the work as optional lesson evidence. Formal mastery is assessed separately.

Worked Example and Independent Practice

Study: To solve 24 ÷ 6, distribute 24 counters among six equal groups. Each group contains four; 6 × 4 checks the answer.

Practice: Draw 18 ÷ 3 as equal groups and write its multiplication check.

Reflect: Explain your reasoning and revise one error. Existing assessments stay separate.
