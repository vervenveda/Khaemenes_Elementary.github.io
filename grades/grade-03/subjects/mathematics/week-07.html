Week 07 · Mathematics

Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading

How can diagrams and data explain motion?

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: data

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through data while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply data; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Mathematics Depth Lab · Day 1: Read fictional ramp-trial distances: 30, 45, 40 cm. Identify the largest and smallest and calculate a 15 cm range.

Proof and revision: Explain your representation, verify each numerical result, identify a likely misconception and correct it. This is practice, not a formal score.

Tuesday: Guided Practice

Focus: geometry

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through geometry while investigating Forces, Motion, and Technical Reading.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply geometry; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Mathematics Depth Lab · Day 2: Represent the distances in a scaled bar graph with a horizontal trial axis and a vertical distance (cm) axis; use equal tick intervals.

Proof and revision: Explain your representation, verify each numerical result, identify a likely misconception and correct it. This is practice, not a formal score.

Week 07 Mathematics Depth Lab · Day 3: Compare two trials using subtraction, then explain why distance alone does not reveal the cause of the differences.

Proof and revision: Explain your representation, verify each numerical result, identify a likely misconception and correct it. This is practice, not a formal score.

Week 07 Mathematics Depth Lab · Day 4: Correct a graph whose axis begins at 35 cm and misleadingly exaggerates differences; discuss transparent scales.

Proof and revision: Explain your representation, verify each numerical result, identify a likely misconception and correct it. This is practice, not a formal score.

Week 07 Mathematics Depth Lab · Day 5: Present an evidence report with the graph, range, one supported conclusion and one missing variable such as ramp height.
