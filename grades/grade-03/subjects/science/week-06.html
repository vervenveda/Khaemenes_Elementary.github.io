Week 06 · Science

Division and Fair Sharing

How do we divide fairly and explain our process?

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: Fossils preserve evidence of past life, such as impressions or traces. A footprint fossil is evidence of an organism's movement, but alone cannot tell us the animal's color.

Independent practice: Draw two fictional fossil impressions. Label what is directly observable and one cautious inference; identify evidence still needed.

Reflect: Explain a choice, correct a mistake and retain optional evidence. Graded mastery remains separate.

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: fossils

Objective: Students strengthen science through fossils while investigating Division and Fair Sharing.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply fossils; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Observe a fictional fossil footprint impression; distinguish the outline seen from guessed animal features.

Explain and revise: Show a drawing, calculation, quotation or observation supporting your reasoning; find a likely misconception and correct it. This is instructional practice, not a replacement assessment.
