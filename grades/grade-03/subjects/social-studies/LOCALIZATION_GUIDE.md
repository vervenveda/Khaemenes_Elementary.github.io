# Grade 3 Social Studies · Local and State Localization Guide

Weeks 4, 11, and 12 are intentionally adaptable. Select one local community/region, the relevant state, and specific Indigenous nation(s)/homelands connected to the region when historically appropriate. Use public sources such as maps, archives, libraries, museums, oral-history collections, newspapers, photographs, historic sites, government records, and tribal sources.

Safeguards: do not require home addresses, immigration/citizenship status, family finances, religion, political affiliation, or private family history. Do not present Indigenous peoples only in the past. Distinguish settlement from prior Indigenous presence/sovereignty. Include change, continuity, benefits, costs, and multiple perspectives.

Florida educators may localize with Florida geography, tribal histories, settlement/migration, civic institutions, landmarks, climate/resources, and state/local archives while retaining the national course structure.
