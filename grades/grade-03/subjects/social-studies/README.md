# Khaemenes Academy · Grade 3 Social Studies
## Communities, Regions & World Connections

Canonical route: `grades/grade-03/subjects/social-studies/index.html`

- 36 discipline-specific weeks / 180 lessons
- 10 new reviewed objective items per week = 360 unique current-week items
- up to 4 spiral-review items added to weekly banks; 7 randomized questions drawn per open-book attempt
- weekly constructed evidence response
- 80% objective mastery unlock policy
- 30-question closed-book randomized midterm after Week 18 from a 180-item pool
- 40-question closed-book cumulative final after Week 36 from a 360-item pool
- corrections + fresh-form reassessment below 80%
- dynamic weekly printable, portfolio evidence, teacher JSON export/import
- Academy Grade 3 learner-scoped subject storage when Family Registry is active
- no external libraries

The legacy 15-point whole-grade assessment remains integrated portfolio evidence, not the formal Social Studies mastery gate.

Teacher convenience code: `KHAE3SS` (not secure authentication).

Scope: inquiry/evidence/local history → U.S. geography/regions → Indigenous nations/local-state history → comparative world communities (Japan, Ghana, Mexico, India) → midterm → Constitution/federalism/elections/civic memory → economics/currencies/trade → historical impact and contributions → culture/human rights/global citizenship/media literacy → research capstone/final/Grade 4 bridge.

## State/local localization

This course follows the Academy-wide **national-core / state-local-overlay** rule. State and local maps, sources, institutions, biographies, Indigenous histories, public services, economic examples, environmental cases, and community evidence should be integrated where appropriate without removing national competencies or changing the 80% mastery standard.

See `STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`.
