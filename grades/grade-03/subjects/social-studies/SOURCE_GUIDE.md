# Grade 3 Social Studies · Teacher Source Guide

Prefer current, authoritative, free sources. Useful families include tribal nation official sites and cultural departments; Smithsonian NMAI; Library of Congress; National Archives; U.S. Census Bureau; USGS; National Park Service; state archives/libraries; local museums/historical societies; official local/state/federal government sites; U.S. Courts/Congress.gov for civics reference; UN/UNESCO for global and human-rights context; World Bank Data and official national statistical agencies for teacher-curated comparative data.

For each important source record creator, title/description, date when known, source type, purpose, relevant evidence, limitations, and citation/location. Do not copy full copyrighted texts/images unless public domain or licensed.
