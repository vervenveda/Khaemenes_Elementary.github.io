# Grade 3 Social Studies · National Portability Crosswalk

## NCSS C3
Questions, disciplinary concepts/tools, source/evidence work, communication, and informed action recur throughout.
https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3

## Florida Grade 3
Covers primary/secondary sources and technology research; African American heroes/patriots; volunteerism, civility, civic virtue, voting/elections, symbols/documents/holidays; supply/demand, scarcity/trade, currencies; U.S. regions/states, North America, map types, scale/distortion, landmarks, climate/resources, settlement patterns, and cultural comparison.
https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/20753/urlt/11-3.pdf

## New York Grade 3
Covers comparative communities around the world, urban/rural variation, government/economy/culture, human rights, prejudice/discrimination, global citizenship/social action, timelines, and comparative geography.
https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/curriculum-instruction/ss-framework-k-8a2.pdf

## California Grade 3
Covers physical/human geography; maps/tables/graphs/photos/charts; local history through primary/community resources; rules/laws/Constitution; local/state/federal/tribal government; branches; civic symbols/documents; historical contributors; and regional economics.
https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/history?c0=4

Formal Social Studies progression requires 80% on every weekly subject gate, the closed-book midterm, and the cumulative final. Constructed responses and portfolio artifacts provide qualitative evidence without letting unrelated subjects mask weak Social Studies mastery.

## State/local localization

State localization is required across the Khaemenes Social Studies sequence. Each grade keeps a broad national disciplinary core while adapting appropriate examples, sources, maps, institutions, Indigenous histories, biographies, civic cases, and community evidence to the learner's state/locality. Localization does not lower the 80% mastery threshold or delete national competencies.

See `STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`.


## State/localization requirement
Grade 3 requires a state/local overlay in local history, U.S. geography/regions, Indigenous nations, local/state government, public figures/institutions, civic action, and the research capstone. The comparative world-community cases remain part of the national core; localization should add state relevance without deleting global comparison or lowering mastery expectations.

See `LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md`.
