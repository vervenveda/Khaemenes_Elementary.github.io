Week 06 · Technology & Tools

Division and Fair Sharing

How do we divide fairly and explain our process?

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Focus: Algorithms for fair distribution.

Study: Define an algorithm as a repeatable series of steps and trace the total after each assignment.

Worked model: Model 12 counters distributed into three boxes, one counter per box each round, yielding four per box.

Independent practice: Write and trace instructions for distributing 20 counters into five groups; verify with multiplication.

Challenge and revision: Debug an algorithm that stops one round early and leaves five counters unassigned.

Evidence and reflection: Create an accessible numbered flow with inputs, steps, output and a test case. This is optional instructional evidence; formal assessments, scores and mastery remain separate.

Monday: Launch and Direct Instruction

Focus: presentations

Objective: Students strengthen technology & tools through presentations while investigating Division and Fair Sharing.

Activity: Model, practice, and apply presentations; students explain thinking with evidence, diagrams, words, numbers, movement, or models.

Evidence: Work sample, oral explanation, teacher observation, correction/revision, and subject mastery evidence.

Depth Lab · Day 1: Trace an algorithm distributing 12 counters into three boxes, recording each round.

Reasoning check: Explain the evidence, identify an error to avoid, and revise your answer. Instructional practice is separate from graded mastery.
