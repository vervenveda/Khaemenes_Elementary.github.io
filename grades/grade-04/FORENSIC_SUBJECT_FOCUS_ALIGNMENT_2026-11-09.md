# Grade 4 — Forensic Subject-Focus Alignment Audit
Date: 2026-11-09
Branch: `audit/elementary-family-report-2026-11-09`
Scope: Original Grade 4 late-year instructional lessons, with targeted sampling across all eight subjects, Weeks 19, 27, 30, 33, 36, and additional high-priority pages in Weeks 19, 21, 28, 29, 31–36.

## Five findings
1. **Mathematics skill drift.** Original Week 27 taught 'angles and geometry' under 'Decimal Fractions and Money'; Week 28 taught 'place value' under measurement; Week 29 'multi-digit multiplication' under geometry; Week 30 'division with remainders' under angle measurement. All four were corrected within their original source pages.
2. **Science theme mismatch.** Week 19 labeled 'energy transfer' under weathering and erosion, Week 32 'Earth materials' under economics, and Week 33 'erosion and landforms' under digital data. Their subject focus, objectives, and teacher cues were realigned to each weekly theme. Cross-disciplinary work remains encouraged.
3. **Language Arts concept drift.** Week 31's generic 'text structure' was refocused on poetry, dramatic dialogue and performance interpretation; Week 34's 'grammar and conventions' was revised to research questions, source notes and evidence-based writing.
4. **Capstone sequencing.** Integrated Projects Week 34 previously emphasized 'peer critique' in the research planning unit. It now emphasizes inquiry planning, source evaluation and evidence design, reserving formal defense and revision for later capstone phases.
5. **Template repetition and regression risk.** The original five-day instructions remain highly templated; this audit corrected high-confidence focus mismatches but is not an exhaustive pedagogical rewrite. Added `tests/grade-four-focus-alignment-forensic.test.cjs` and a dedicated workflow to prevent those ten source errors from silently returning.

## Remaining limitations
- Sampling does not establish that every remaining Grade 4 focus is aligned. A second full 144-page semantic inspection is advisable.
- Replacing focus cues does not by itself create new worked subject-specific examples. The previously added late-year studio remains supplemental.
- Specialist Social Studies subject course uses a distinct assessment and record system; do not merge it into grade-wide mastery.
- Source attribution, curriculum depth, accessibility, printing, family routing, and adult authorization require real browser and expert review.

No live deployment and no real learner-record mutations occurred.
