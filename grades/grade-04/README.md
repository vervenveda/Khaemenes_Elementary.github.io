# Khaemenes Academy Fourth Grade — Subject-Based 36 Week A++ Curriculum

This folder contains the complete Grade 04 program for Khaemenes Elementary.

## Curriculum Inventory

- 36 weeks
- 8 subject halls
- 5 lesson blocks per subject per week
- 1,440 subject lesson blocks
- 36 weekly plans
- 36 printable packets
- 36 weekly A++ mastery assessments
- Midyear demonstration / midterm
- Final readiness demonstration / final exam
- Teacher / Family Tools
- Learner-scoped local course records
- Portfolio evidence
- 80% completion certification

## Unified Academy Architecture

Grade 04 follows the shared Elementary authority model:

```text
Academy Family Registry → Elementary continuity → NAIB → Archaemenes → Grade 04 course state
```

- Academy Family Registry owns learner identity.
- Archaemenes is the current Elementary mentor.
- Young Scholar is the Grade 04 presentation mode.
- Grade 04 owns its curriculum, assessment evidence, progress, and certificate state.
- Mentor guidance does not award mastery.
- The student landing page does not accept direct mastery-score entry.
- Teacher / Family Tools records verified assessment results after evidence review.

## Record Model

The former shared browser record remains a compatibility source only. When an eligible Grade 04 learner is active, legacy Grade 04 state may be copied non-destructively into a learner-scoped record.

## Certification Rule

Certificate readiness requires:

- active eligible Grade 04 learner;
- all 36 weekly mastery results at 80% or higher;
- midterm at 80% or higher;
- final at 80% or higher;
- portfolio evidence approved.
