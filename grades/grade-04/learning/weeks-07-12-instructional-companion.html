Supplementary offline learning with worked examples and independent reasoning. Existing course assessments, 80% mastery, records and certificates remain authoritative. This companion saves no learner data.

Week 07 · Long division and estimates

Reading and reasoning

Explain why 936 ÷ 24 = 39: 24×40=960 is too high, while 24×39=936. Check the quotient by multiplication.

Mathematical modeling

Distribute 938 items among 24 groups: 39 per group with 2 left. Discuss what the remainder means in context.

Investigation

Distinguish an observation, estimate, calculation and conclusion in a sample report.

Support: use a worked model and a diagram before independent explanation. Challenge: critique a plausible but incorrect solution and defend a correction.

Week 12 · Geography and maps
