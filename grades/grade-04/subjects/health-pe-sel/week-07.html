Week 07 · Health, PE & SEL

Long Division, Estimation, and Problem Solving

How do mathematicians decide what a remainder means?

Monday: Direct Instruction and Vocabulary

Focus: leadership

Objective: Students strengthen health, pe & sel through leadership while investigating 'Long Division, Estimation, and Problem Solving'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model expert thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Health, PE & SEL A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about long division, estimation, and problem solving.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Health, PE & SEL. Connect to the essential question: How do mathematicians decide what a remainder means? Model the focus skill: leadership. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, or clear explanation. Close with a revision or reflection: I improved my thinking by ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1: Design a cooperative supply-moving plan with safe loads, breaks, and roles tailored to participant choice. Explain key ideas and show one worked model.

Evidence and revision check: Record a source, model, calculation or safely accessible choice. These optional activities do not change formal mastery.

Week 07 Applied Worked Studio · Day 1: Design safe supply transport roles with choices for seated or standing work.

Evidence criteria: Show a labeled model or safe demonstration, explain one decision, and correct one error before presenting. This is formative practice, not an official score.
