Week 07 · Integrated Projects

Long Division, Estimation, and Problem Solving

How do mathematicians decide what a remainder means?

Monday: Direct Instruction and Vocabulary

Focus: peer critique

Objective: Students strengthen integrated projects through peer critique while investigating 'Long Division, Estimation, and Problem Solving'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model expert thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Integrated Projects A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about long division, estimation, and problem solving.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Integrated Projects. Connect to the essential question: How do mathematicians decide what a remainder means? Model the focus skill: peer critique. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, or clear explanation. Close with a revision or reflection: I improved my thinking by ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1: Develop a school-material transport plan for 95 objects in containers of 8; justify a 12-container plan using arithmetic. Explain key ideas and show one worked model.

Evidence and revision check: Record a source, model, calculation or safely accessible choice. These optional activities do not change formal mastery.

Week 07 Project Worked Studio · Day 1 — Worked model: 95 supplies grouped in eights gives 11 full groups and 7 extra: 8 × 11 + 7 = 95.
