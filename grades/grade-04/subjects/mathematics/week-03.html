Week 03 · Mathematics

Theme, Character, and Multi-Digit Addition/Subtraction

How do stories and strategies reveal change over time?

Worked Example · Evidence and Practice

Study: For 4,386 + 2,745, combine ones and regroup: 6+5=11; tens 8+4+1=13; hundreds 3+7+1=11; thousands 4+2+1=7, giving 7,131.

Independent task: Solve 5,278 + 1,694 and verify by subtraction.

Evidence check: Explain a step, revise an error, and save optional ungraded practice. Formal assessments remain unchanged.
