Week 06 · Mathematics

Division Foundations and Procedural Writing

How do we explain a process so another person can follow it?

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: Division is the inverse of multiplication: 84 ÷ 7 = 12 because 7 × 12 = 84. Show 84 counters in 7 equal groups or use an area model.

Independent practice: Find another rectangle with area 84 m² and compare perimeters.

Reflect: Explain, revise, and preserve optional evidence. Assessment and mastery systems remain separate.

Monday: Direct Instruction and Vocabulary

Focus: fraction operations

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through fraction operations while investigating 'Division Foundations and Procedural Writing'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model expert thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about division foundations and procedural writing.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How do we explain a process so another person can follow it? Model the focus skill: fraction operations. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, or clear explanation. Close with a revision or reflection: I improved my thinking by ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Academic Depth Lab · Day 1: Model 84 ÷ 7 = 12 with equal groups and verify 7 × 12 = 84.

Explain and revise: Show a drawing, calculation, quotation or observation supporting your reasoning; find a likely misconception and correct it. This is instructional practice, not a replacement assessment.
