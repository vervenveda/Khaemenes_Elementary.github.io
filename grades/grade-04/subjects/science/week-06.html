Week 06 · Science

Division Foundations and Procedural Writing

How do we explain a process so another person can follow it?

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: Erosion moves weathered soil or rock through water, wind, ice or gravity. A river can carry sediment and later deposit it when water slows; a single observation does not identify every cause.

Independent practice: Diagram an imaginary stream before and after rainfall, labeling erosion and deposition. State two variables that could alter the pattern.

Reflect: Explain, revise, and preserve optional evidence. Assessment and mastery systems remain separate.
