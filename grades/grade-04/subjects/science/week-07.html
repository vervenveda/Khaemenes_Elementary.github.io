Week 07 · Science

Long Division, Estimation, and Problem Solving

How do mathematicians decide what a remainder means?

Monday: Direct Instruction and Vocabulary

Focus: fair testing, variables and evidence

Objective: Students strengthen science through fair testing, variables and evidence while investigating 'Long Division, Estimation, and Problem Solving'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model expert thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Science A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about long division, estimation, and problem solving.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Science. Connect to the essential question: How do mathematicians decide what a remainder means? Model the focus skill: fair testing, variables and evidence. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, or clear explanation. Close with a revision or reflection: I improved my thinking by ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
