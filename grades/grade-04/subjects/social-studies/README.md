Canonical route: `grades/grade-04/subjects/social-studies/index.html`

- 12 new reviewed objective items per week = 432 unique current-week items
- up to 4 spiral-review records added to weekly banks
- 8 randomized questions per open-book weekly attempt
- weekly constructed evidence response requiring claim + 3 pieces of evidence + reasoning
- 80% objective mastery unlock policy

- correction + fresh-form reassessment below 80%

- no external libraries
