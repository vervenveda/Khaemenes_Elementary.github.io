# Grade 4 Social Studies · Teacher Source Guide

Prioritize free, authoritative, contextualized sources:
- official tribal nation sites/cultural departments;
- Smithsonian National Museum of the American Indian;
- Library of Congress;
- National Archives;
- state archives, state libraries, local libraries, museums, historical societies;
- U.S. Census Bureau, USGS, National Park Service;
- official local/state government sites, state constitutions, legislatures, courts, election offices;
- Congress.gov and U.S. Courts for federal civics reference;
- Federal Reserve education resources for age-appropriate economics/financial literacy;
- official statistical/economic agencies for teacher-curated data.

For each source record creator, title/description, date, source type, purpose/audience, relevant evidence, limitations, and citation/location. Open full sources instead of relying on snippets. Link or briefly quote lawful material; do not copy full copyrighted works unless licensed/public domain.
