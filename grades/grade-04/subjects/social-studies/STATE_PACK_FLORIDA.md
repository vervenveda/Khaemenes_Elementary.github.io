# Grade 4 Social Studies · Florida State Pack

This pack supplies optional Florida-specific evidence prompts for the required state/local checkpoints while preserving the national Grade 4 course and 80% mastery gates.

Use age-appropriate Florida maps, tribal nation/community sources, state/local archives, museums, historic sites, government records, economic data, and civic institutions. Strong Grade 4 connections include Florida geography and hazards; Seminole and Miccosukee present-day communities and earlier Indigenous histories; St. Augustine, Fort Mose and colonial periods; territorial/statehood history; Seminole removal/resistance; Florida Civil War/Reconstruction connections; transportation, agriculture, tourism, migration, civil-rights history, environment, state government, and contemporary economy.

Record the actual source or case used in the learner's localization-evidence field. This pack adds evidence; it does not replace national-core competencies.
