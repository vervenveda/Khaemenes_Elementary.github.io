# Grade 4 Social Studies · State Pack Template

Status: **template**

Populate the universal state-pack schema with state geography; specific Indigenous nations and present-day communities; local/state chronology; government/constitution and civic institutions; African American and other local contributors; economy; archives/museums; student-facing source sets; and checkpoint substitutions.

A state pack adds jurisdiction-specific evidence to the canonical course. It may not lower the 80% mastery threshold or delete national competencies.
