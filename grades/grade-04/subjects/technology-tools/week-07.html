Week 07 · Technology & Tools

Long Division, Estimation, and Problem Solving

How do mathematicians decide what a remainder means?

Monday: Direct Instruction and Vocabulary

Focus: data tables, division algorithms and checking remainders

Objective: Students strengthen technology & tools through data tables, division algorithms and checking remainders while investigating 'Long Division, Estimation, and Problem Solving'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model expert thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Technology & Tools A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about long division, estimation, and problem solving.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Technology & Tools. Connect to the essential question: How do mathematicians decide what a remainder means? Model the focus skill: data tables, division algorithms and checking remainders. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, or clear explanation. Close with a revision or reflection: I improved my thinking by ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1 — Model and vocabulary: Create a spreadsheet-style planning table for 95 supplies in boxes of 8 and verify 11 full boxes with 7 left. Identify the goal, define two essential terms and model one concrete step.

Evidence and revision check: Explain one decision and show the revision. This optional practice does not change mastery or formal scores.

Week 07 Applied Worked Studio · Day 1: Make a table with 95 supplies, capacity 8 per box and 11 full boxes.

Evidence criteria: Show a labeled model or safe demonstration, explain one decision, and correct one error before presenting. This is formative practice, not an official score.
