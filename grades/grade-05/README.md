# Khaemenes Academy Fifth Grade — Subject-Based 36 Week A++ Curriculum

Fifth Grade is the final Elementary course in Khaemenes Academy and the bridge into Middle School readiness.

## Curriculum

- 36 weeks
- 8 subject halls
- 5 lesson blocks per subject per week
- 1,440 subject lesson blocks
- 36 weekly plans
- 36 printable packets
- 36 weekly mastery assessments
- midterm
- final
- portfolio / capstone evidence
- teacher and family tools
- middle-school readiness certification
- advanced Grade 06 preview bridge

## Unified Academy architecture

```text
Academy Family Registry
  → NAIB intake / resource direction / delegation
  → Khaemenes Academy
  → Archaemenes · Young Scholar
  → Grade 05 course state
  → assessment evidence
  → adult verification
  → certificate / Middle School readiness
```

Family Registry owns learner identity and formal placement. NAIB delegates learners to the appropriate Academy or ecosystem destination. Khaemenes Academy provides Archaemenes as the institutional mentor. Grade 05 owns Grade 05 progress and certification. The mentor does not award mastery.

Formal Grade 05 records are learner-scoped. The older browser key `khaemenes_grade5_subject_36_aplusplus_v1` remains only as a compatibility/migration source for existing local records.

## Certification

The Fifth Grade certificate requires:

- an active eligible `grade-05` Academy learner;
- all 36 weekly mastery results at 80% or above;
- midterm at 80% or above;
- final at 80% or above;
- approved portfolio / capstone evidence.

The landing page is learner-facing and read-only for mastery. Verified results are entered through Teacher / Family Tools after assessment evidence is reviewed.

## Continuity into Middle School

Completion of Grade 05 records Fifth Grade achievement and readiness evidence. It does not silently change the learner's Academy stage or create a Middle School identity. Transition into Grade 06 remains an explicit Academy/family placement action.

The Grade 05 landing page now contains a dedicated Middle School bridge with two distinct pathways:

### Advanced Grade 06 Preview

Advanced learners may explore selected Grade 06 curriculum and Middle School expectations before formal promotion. Preview access is enrichment only. It does not:

- change `stage: elementary`;
- change `grade: grade-05`;
- create a Grade 06 formal record;
- unlock Grade 06 certification;
- bypass the Academy/family placement decision.

The preview is particularly useful for the next-step strands that become more explicit in Grade 06:

- ratios, rates, percent, rational numbers, expressions, equations, and statistics;
- formal citation, argument analysis, disciplinary literacy, and longer research writing;
- claim-evidence-reasoning, broader Middle School systems science, and engineering analysis;
- World Languages & Global Culture as a dedicated subject hall;
- stronger executive-function and independent research systems.

### Formal Grade 06 Transition

Grade 05 certification provides readiness evidence. Formal Middle School placement is still handled deliberately through the Academy/family layer. Once the learner is formally placed at `stage: middle` and `grade: grade-06`, the hardened Grade 06 learner-scoped record becomes eligible.

## Grade 05 → Grade 06 Academic Fit

The transition is academically coherent:

- **Language Arts:** Grade 05 accurate quotation, comparison, essays, research, and revision lead into Grade 06 citation, central-idea analysis, argument evaluation, and disciplinary literacy.
- **Mathematics:** Grade 05 decimals, fractions, coordinate planes, volume, and multi-step problem solving lead into Grade 06 ratios/rates, percent, rational numbers, expressions/equations, inequalities, geometry, and statistics.
- **Science:** Grade 05 matter, Earth systems, ecosystems, models, and evidence lead into Grade 06 physical/life/Earth systems, data analysis, engineering, and CER.
- **Social Studies:** Grade 05 civics and historical foundations transition into Grade 06 world geography, ancient civilizations, primary sources, economics, trade, migration, and comparative culture.
- **Technology / Projects:** Grade 05 files, spreadsheets, algorithms, source notes, presentations, and integrated capstones directly support Grade 06 independent research and design expectations.
- **World Languages:** Grade 06 adds a dedicated strand; the advanced preview introduces it without changing formal placement.
