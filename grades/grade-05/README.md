# Khaemenes Academy Fifth Grade — Subject-Based 36 Week A++ Curriculum

Fifth Grade is the final Elementary course in Khaemenes Academy and the bridge into Middle School readiness.

## Curriculum

- 36 weeks
- 8 subject halls
- 5 lesson blocks per subject per week
- 1,440 subject lesson blocks
- 36 weekly plans
- 36 printable packets
- 36 weekly mastery assessments
- midterm
- final
- portfolio / capstone evidence
- teacher and family tools
- middle-school readiness certification

## Unified Academy architecture

```text
Academy Family Registry
  → Elementary continuity
  → NAIB
  → Archaemenes · Young Scholar
  → Grade 05 course state
  → assessment evidence
  → adult verification
  → certificate
```

Family Registry owns learner identity. Archaemenes mentors. Grade 05 owns Grade 05 progress and certification. The mentor does not award mastery.

Formal Grade 05 records are learner-scoped. The older browser key `khaemenes_grade5_subject_36_aplusplus_v1` remains only as a compatibility/migration source for existing local records.

## Certification

The Fifth Grade certificate requires:

- an active eligible `grade-05` Academy learner;
- all 36 weekly mastery results at 80% or above;
- midterm at 80% or above;
- final at 80% or above;
- approved portfolio / capstone evidence.

The landing page is learner-facing and read-only for mastery. Verified results are entered through Teacher / Family Tools after assessment evidence is reviewed.

## Continuity into Middle School

Completion of Grade 05 records Fifth Grade achievement and readiness evidence. It does not silently change the learner's Academy stage or create a Middle School identity. Transition into Grade 06 remains an explicit Academy/family action.
