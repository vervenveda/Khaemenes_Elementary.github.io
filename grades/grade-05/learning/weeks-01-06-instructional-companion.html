Week 01 · Scholar launch and evidence

Evidence-based literacy

Read a claim about a school garden and separate observations, predictions and opinions. Draft a paragraph with two attributed supporting details.

Worked mathematical reasoning

Represent 60,407.305 as 60,000+400+7+0.3+0.005. Explain the zero placeholders and check digit values.

Scientific or practical investigation

Design a testable question about seed growth using two comparable fictional trials; identify controls and avoid claiming a prediction is a result.

Support: Offer a worked model, visual representation and sentence frames. Challenge: Analyze an incorrect solution, defend an alternative and state the limits of available evidence.

Week 06 · Long division and remainder decisions

Evidence-based literacy

Explain why interpreting a remainder requires context, not just applying the same rounding rule.

Worked mathematical reasoning

Distribute 1,258 books into boxes of 24. 24×52=1,248 and remainder 10; 52 full boxes are possible, but moving all books needs 53 boxes.

Scientific or practical investigation

Model the problem with a table. Compare boxing all books with allocating complete sets, and defend each different remainder decision.

Support: Offer a worked model, visual representation and sentence frames. Challenge: Analyze an incorrect solution, defend an alternative and state the limits of available evidence.
