Week 25 · Revolution foundations and civic arguments

Source analysis

Compare two fictional accounts of a colonial protest; distinguish direct testimony, later interpretation, and whose voices are missing.

Worked quantitative reasoning

A fictional petition receives 168 signatures over 7 days: mean 168÷7=24 signatures per day. Explain why the daily count may vary.

Independent inquiry

Write a source ledger listing author, date, audience and evidence; consider Indigenous, enslaved and other marginalized perspectives.

Support: Annotate a source and work through one model together. Challenge: Identify a missing perspective, challenge an assumption, and defend a reasoned revision.

Week 28 · Research: question to thesis

Source analysis

Turn 'How do communities save water?' into a specific investigable question with measurable outcomes and appropriate sources.

Worked quantitative reasoning

A sample uses 2.4 L per day for 15 days: total 36 L. If a model reduces use by 0.3 L daily, savings over 15 days are 4.5 L.

Independent inquiry

Plan a safe comparison using identical observation periods; document source provenance and avoid presenting hypothetical results as observed.
