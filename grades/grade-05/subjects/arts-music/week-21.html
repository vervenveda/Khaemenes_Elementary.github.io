Week 21 · Arts & Music

Sun, Earth, Moon, and Patterns

How do patterns in the sky help us explain time and motion?

Monday: Direct Instruction and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: media messages

Objective: Students strengthen arts & music through media messages while investigating 'Sun, Earth, Moon, and Patterns'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model advanced thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Arts & Music A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about sun, earth, moon, and patterns.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Arts & Music. Connect to the essential question: How do patterns in the sky help us explain time and motion? Model the focus skill: media messages. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
