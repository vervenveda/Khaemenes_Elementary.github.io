Week 06 · Mathematics

Long Division and Remainder Decisions

How do we decide what a remainder means in context?

Evidence Studio · Week 06

Study: When 53 students ride in vans holding 8 each, 53 ÷ 8 = 6 remainder 5. Six vans are not enough: seven vans are needed if everyone rides. Different contexts can call for rounding up, a leftover, or a fractional answer.

Independent practice: Model 4.8 ÷ 0.6 and explain why scaling both values preserves the quotient.

Reflect: Identify an assumption, correct one error, and retain optional evidence. Formal assessment and mastery stay unchanged.
