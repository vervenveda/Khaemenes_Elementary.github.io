Week 07 · Mathematics

Fractions with Unlike Denominators

How can unlike parts be made comparable?

Monday: Direct Instruction and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: geometry classification

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through geometry classification while investigating 'Fractions with Unlike Denominators'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model advanced thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about fractions with unlike denominators.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How can unlike parts be made comparable? Model the focus skill: geometry classification. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Mathematics Depth Lab · Day 1: Use fraction strips to compare 1/2 and 1/3 by converting to sixths: 3/6 and 2/6.

Proof and revision: Explain your representation, verify each numerical result, identify a likely misconception and correct it. This is practice, not a formal score.
