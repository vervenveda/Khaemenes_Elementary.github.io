# Grade 5 Social Studies · National Portability Crosswalk

Grade 5 varies by state, so Khaemenes uses a strong national U.S./North American history core plus required state/local localization and a Western Hemisphere comparison layer.

## NCSS C3
Questions/inquiry, disciplinary tools, sourcing/evidence, claims/counterclaims, communication, and informed civic reasoning recur throughout.
https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3

## Florida Grade 5
The course covers Florida's Grade 5 expectations in historical inquiry; pre-Columbian American societies and Indigenous regional diversity; exploration technology/motives/routes; interactions among Indigenous, African, English, French, Dutch, and Spanish peoples; colonial regional comparison; slavery and Atlantic trade; Revolution and founding documents; Confederation/Northwest Ordinance; Constitution; Louisiana Purchase, War of 1812, transportation/communication, westward expansion and Indigenous impacts; geography/latitude-longitude/states and territories; market economy, trade and technology; and civics/representative government/Bill of Rights.
https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/20602/urlt/10-3.pdf

## California Grade 5
California Grade 5 is “United States History and Geography: Making a New Nation,” emphasizing pre-Columbian societies, exploration, colonial settlement, the Revolution, Constitution, westward movement, slavery, Indigenous conflict/dispossession, geography, and development of the republic through roughly 1850. The Khaemenes spine directly supports that arc while adding source-literacy and comparative depth.
https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/documents/histsocscistnd.pdf

## New York Grade 5
New York emphasizes the United States, Canada, and Latin America, including geography, Indigenous peoples, history, cultural diversity, government, economics, and contemporary comparison. Khaemenes preserves the deep U.S. history spine while adding dedicated Canada, Mexico/Latin America, and hemispheric-interdependence weeks.
https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/sscore1.pdf

## State/localization requirement
State/local examples are required at designated checkpoints. They may add or substitute case studies, maps, source sets, institutions, state-history connections, Indigenous nation evidence, and capstone topics—but may not delete the national core or reduce the Academy's 80% mastery threshold.
