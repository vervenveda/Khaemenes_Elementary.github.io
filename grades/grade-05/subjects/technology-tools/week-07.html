Week 07 · Technology & Tools

Fractions with Unlike Denominators

How can unlike parts be made comparable?

Monday: Direct Instruction and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: fraction models, equivalent units and calculation checks

Objective: Students strengthen technology & tools through fraction models, equivalent units and calculation checks while investigating 'Fractions with Unlike Denominators'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model advanced thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Technology & Tools A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about fractions with unlike denominators.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Technology & Tools. Connect to the essential question: How can unlike parts be made comparable? Model the focus skill: fraction models, equivalent units and calculation checks. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Week 07 Practical Depth Lab · Day 1: Use fraction strips to compare 1/2 and 1/3, then document equivalent sixths and a checked sum of 5/6. Explain key ideas and show one worked model.

Evidence and revision check: Record a source, model, calculation or safely accessible choice. These optional activities do not change formal mastery.

Week 07 Applied Worked Studio · Day 1: Model 1/2 = 3/6 and 1/3 = 2/6 using equal-sized strips.

Evidence criteria: Show a labeled model or safe demonstration, explain one decision, and correct one error before presenting. This is formative practice, not an official score.
