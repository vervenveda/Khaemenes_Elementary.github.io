Health, PE & SEL · Week 31

Literature Circles and Theater

team collaboration

Monday: Health: team collaboration

Objective: Students build health skill through team collaboration within the weekly theme Literature Circles and Theater.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for team collaboration.

Teacher script: Introduce Health in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing health progress.
