Final Review and Celebration

grade 3 readiness habits

Monday: Health: grade 3 readiness habits

Objective: Students build health skill through grade 3 readiness habits within the weekly theme Final Review and Celebration.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for grade 3 readiness habits.

Teacher script: Introduce Health in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing health progress.
