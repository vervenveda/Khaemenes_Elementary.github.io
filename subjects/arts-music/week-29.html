Arts & Music · Week 29

Government, Laws, and Problem Solving

public service poster

Monday: Visual Art: public service poster

Objective: Students build visual art skill through public service poster within the weekly theme Government, Laws, and Problem Solving.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for public service poster.

Teacher script: Introduce Visual Art in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing visual art progress.
