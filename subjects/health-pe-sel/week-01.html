Health, PE & SEL · Week 01

Scholar Routines and Reading Stamina

routines, safety, and feelings

Monday: Health: routines, safety, and feelings

Objective: Students build health skill through routines, safety, and feelings within the weekly theme Scholar Routines and Reading Stamina.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for routines, safety, and feelings.

Teacher script: Introduce Health in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing health progress.
