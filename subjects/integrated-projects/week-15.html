Weather and Seasonal Patterns

weather station report

Monday: Research: weather station report

Objective: Students build research skill through weather station report within the weekly theme Weather and Seasonal Patterns.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for weather station report.

Teacher script: Introduce Research in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing research progress.
