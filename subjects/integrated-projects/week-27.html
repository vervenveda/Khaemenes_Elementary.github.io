Monday: Research: word-pattern field guide

Objective: Students build research skill through word-pattern field guide within the weekly theme Advanced Phonics and Spelling Patterns.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for word-pattern field guide.

Teacher script: Introduce Research in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.
