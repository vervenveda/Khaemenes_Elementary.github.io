Language Arts · Week 35

Second Grade Capstone

portfolio writing and presentation

Monday: Reading: portfolio writing and presentation

Objective: Students build reading skill through portfolio writing and presentation within the weekly theme Second Grade Capstone.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for portfolio writing and presentation.

Teacher script: Introduce Reading in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing reading progress.
