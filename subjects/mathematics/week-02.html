numbers to 100 and ten-frames

Monday: Operations: numbers to 100 and ten-frames

Objective: Students build operations skill through numbers to 100 and ten-frames within the weekly theme Long Vowels, Number Fluency, and Careful Observation.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for numbers to 100 and ten-frames.

Teacher script: Introduce Operations in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.
