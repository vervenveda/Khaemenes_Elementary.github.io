Fractions and Fair Sharing

equal shares and partitioning

Monday: Operations: equal shares and partitioning

Objective: Students build operations skill through equal shares and partitioning within the weekly theme Fractions and Fair Sharing.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for equal shares and partitioning.

Teacher script: Introduce Operations in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing operations progress.
