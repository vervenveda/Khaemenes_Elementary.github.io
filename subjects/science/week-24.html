Science · Week 24

Telling Time and Calendars

sky patterns and observations

Monday: Observation: sky patterns and observations

Objective: Students build observation skill through sky patterns and observations within the weekly theme Telling Time and Calendars.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for sky patterns and observations.

Teacher script: Introduce Observation in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.

Evidence: Work sample or oral demonstration showing observation progress.
