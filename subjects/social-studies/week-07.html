food, farms, and communities

Monday: Civics: food, farms, and communities

Objective: Students build civics skill through food, farms, and communities within the weekly theme Plant Life Cycles and Data.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for food, farms, and communities.

Teacher script: Introduce Civics in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.
