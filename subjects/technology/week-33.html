Monday: Digital Citizenship: algorithms and loops

Objective: Students build digital citizenship skill through algorithms and loops within the weekly theme Technology, Coding, and Communication.

Activity: Model, practice, discuss, and create evidence for algorithms and loops.

Teacher script: Introduce Digital Citizenship in student-friendly language. Model one clear example and think aloud. Students practice with talk, tools, writing, drawing, movement, or design. Close with one evidence sentence or demonstration.
